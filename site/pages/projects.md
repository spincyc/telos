# Projects

The directory is organized by subject first and AI provider second. Provider
editions share evidence, not templates: each can choose the sequence, document
count, and presentation best suited to its subject.

## Field

- [Lake Country Fishing](projects/lake-country-fishing/index.md) — Claude and
  ChatGPT editions for Pine Lake and North Lake.

## Science and workshop

- [Electricity & Magnetism](projects/electricity/index.md) — two independently
  structured routes from safe experiments to a clearly marked danger boundary.
- [Potato Launcher](projects/potato-launcher/index.md) — two safety-first
  treatments of combustion, fabrication, and projectile physics.

## Systems

- [Homelab](projects/homelab/index.md) — provider-neutral engineering record.
  No ChatGPT parallel is published.

## How editions relate

An edition owns its source tree, PDFs, landing page, and source-selection
manifest. Research lives outside every edition and can be extended by any
contributor. This deliberately avoids a lowest-common-denominator page schema.

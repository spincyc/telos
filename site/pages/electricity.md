# Electricity & Magnetism

A hands-on course for teenagers that builds, lesson by lesson, to a working
spark-gap Tesla coil.

Every lesson exists because the coil at the end will not work unless you
understand the thing the lesson teaches. A Tesla coil is a pair of coupled
resonant circuits fed by a spark gap, and each of those words is a lesson. The
order is not negotiable.

Each lesson opens with a prediction to commit to before anything is switched
on, is built by the students rather than demonstrated to them, produces a
measurement, and only then arrives at the equation.

- [Course Map](doc/electricity/overview.pdf) — the through-line, who does what, and the machine you are building

## Safety

Lessons 1 through 9 run from batteries and small plug-in supplies and cannot
hurt anybody. From Lesson 10 the apparatus contains mains-derived voltages that
kill people every year, and the course is written on the assumption that an
adult owns the mains side of it permanently.

- [**Safety**](doc/electricity/safety.pdf) — the rules that do not bend

It is read start to finish before Lesson 10, not during it, and posted on the
wall where the coil is built.

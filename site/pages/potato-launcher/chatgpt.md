# Potato Launcher · ChatGPT edition

An independently organized, safety-first field set for a supervised physics
project. It begins with stop gates and range command, then moves backward into
the models and workmanship evidence. This is deliberately not a page-for-page
copy of the Claude edition.

> **Supervised only.** One named sober adult controls the launcher, fuel,
> ignition, commands, misfires, and cease-fire. A plumbing pressure mark is not
> manufacturer approval for combustion-launcher service. Confirm law, site
> rules, fire conditions, and landowner permission before assembly or use.

## Print this set

- [Range Command Card](../../../doc/potato-launcher/chatgpt/field-guide.pdf) — the
  no-go gates, four roles, spoken shot states, cease-fire triggers, and the
  fixed 60-second misfire hold.
- [Science Notebook](../../../doc/potato-launcher/chatgpt/science-notebook.pdf) —
  bounded combustion, the limits of pressure markings, ideal versus real
  projectile flight, and evidence tables.
- [Build Standard & Inspection Record](../../../doc/potato-launcher/chatgpt/build-standard.pdf)
  — pressure-component screening, inspectable hold points, the
  **48-hours-or-manufacturer-longer** cure lock, retirement criteria, and
  forbidden pneumatic conversions.

The layout is compact, monochrome, and designed for home printing. Keep the
Range Command Card on the firing line.

## Research provenance

This edition uses the project’s provider-neutral research library. Atomic
claims are drawn from NASA, NIST, OSHA, NIOSH, the U.S. Fire Administration,
the National Park Service, and manufacturer technical literature from
Charlotte Pipe and Oatey. Its selection manifest states exactly which shared
claims were considered salient and where they appear. The prose, organization,
and editorial judgments are original to the ChatGPT edition; no source claims
that a homemade PVC combustion launcher is certified safe.

The governing constraints do not bend: solid-wall pressure-marked pipe **and**
pressure fittings only; never DWV or cellular/foam core; never compressed air,
bottled gas, oxygen enrichment, or liquid accelerant; cure at least 48 hours
and longer when the product instructions require; retire damaged material; use
impact-rated eye protection and double hearing protection; and fire only into
a walked, empty range with a positive earthen backstop.

# Potato Launcher

This project treats a combustion launcher as supervised physics and disciplined
fabrication—not as a toy. The editions share safety research while organizing
the learning and build record independently.

## Claude edition

Four physics lessons, six craft sheets, a complete build, a build log, and a
flame-acoustics demonstration.

[Browse the Claude edition](claude/index.md)

## ChatGPT edition

A newly researched build dossier organized around go/no-go gates, material
limits, measurement, and a controlled firing-day protocol.

[Browse the ChatGPT edition](chatgpt/index.md)

> Adult supervision, eye and hearing protection, an empty downrange area, and
> refusal to use damaged or unsuitable pressure components are non-negotiable.

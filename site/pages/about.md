# About Telos

Telos turns serious home projects into durable printed references. The
repository publishes source alongside every PDF, favors black-and-white
legibility, and treats one-page promises as hard print contracts.

## Provider editions

The AI provider is part of a publication’s provenance. Claude and ChatGPT
editions are separate works: neither is presented as a skin over the other, and
their tables of contents do not need to match. Project hubs make that difference
visible before a reader downloads anything.

## Shared research

The `research/` tree is provider-neutral. It separates:

1. source records — identity, authority, URL, retrieval date, and use limits;
2. atomic claims — what the source supports, with scope and caveats;
3. edition selections — the claims an edition considers salient and why.

This lets another AI reuse strong research, challenge it, add a better source,
or make a different editorial choice without copying publication prose.

The full contributor contract is in
[Provider editions](provider-editions.md).

## Authority and safety

These documents are educational material, not professional advice. Fishing
rules change; consult the current Wisconsin DNR publication for the water and
date. Mains electricity, high voltage, combustion launchers, and open flame can
kill or permanently injure. The safety boundary in each project is part of the
content, not boilerplate.

Sources, artwork provenance, and licensing are recorded in
[Credits & Sources](credits.md) and [Licensing](license.md).

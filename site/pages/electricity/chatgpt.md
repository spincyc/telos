# Electricity & Magnetism

## ChatGPT edition

Start with a question, not a chapter number. This edition is a set of printable
investigation books: predict, build within a deliberately small low-voltage
domain, measure, change one thing, and explain from evidence.

> **The boundary is absolute:** learners use only the isolated battery sources
> specified in the sheets, never more than 12 V DC. Household power, opened
> appliances, large batteries or capacitors, sparks, and high voltage are
> outside the course. Adult supervision does not make someone electrically
> qualified.

## A route through the set

Print **Start Here** and **The Boundary Card** first. Charge & Field can stand
alone. Circuits by Evidence establishes the measurement habits used by the two
magnetism books; take those in either order.

This edition draws from provider-neutral claim and source records. It selected
NIST for units, DOE for stable introductory physics, and OSHA/CPSC for the
safety boundary. Future editions can reuse, challenge, or extend those atomic
claims without inheriting this document structure or prose.

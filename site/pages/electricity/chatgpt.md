# Electricity & Magnetism

## ChatGPT edition

This is a complete, independently researched course: ten measured lessons, a
low-voltage synthesis build, and five demonstrations. It follows the same broad
learning journey as the Claude edition without sharing its prose, diagrams, or
page architecture.

> **The boundary is absolute:** learners use only the isolated battery sources
> specified in the sheets, never more than 12 V DC. Household power, opened
> appliances, large batteries or charged power capacitors, sparks, and exposed
> high voltage are outside learner work. An adult facilitator is not thereby an
> electrically qualified person.

## Build after Lesson 9

[Build the Coupled-Coil Instrument](../../../doc/electricity/chatgpt/build-the-coil.pdf)
combines current-produced field, induction, geometry, and measurement in an
isolated AA-cell platform. It deliberately does not produce sparks and is not a
Tesla coil.

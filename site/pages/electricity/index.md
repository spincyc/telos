# Electricity & Magnetism

Two editions teach the same physical world through different sequences. Both
begin with learner-owned low-voltage experiments and mark the point where
dangerous work becomes permanently adult-owned.

## Claude edition

Eleven cumulative lessons and five demonstrations build toward a spark-gap
Tesla coil.

## ChatGPT edition

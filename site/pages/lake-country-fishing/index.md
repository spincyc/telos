# Lake Country Fishing

Two independent editions share a research library about Pine Lake, North Lake,
their fish, and Wisconsin practice. Choose the editorial approach that fits the
day you are planning.

## Claude edition

An encyclopedic field library: lake maps, species cards, individual rig sheets,
cooking guides, reference sheets, and complete per-lake compendia.

[Browse the Claude edition](claude/index.md)

## ChatGPT edition

A newly researched, task-led field system organized around choosing water,
season, target, presentation, and safe handling.

[Browse the ChatGPT edition](chatgpt/index.md)

> Neither edition states current law. Check the Wisconsin DNR’s current
> regulations for the specific water and date before fishing.

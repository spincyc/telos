# ChatGPT edition selection manifest

Edition: ChatGPT
Revision: 2026-07-26

This manifest records which provider-neutral claims the ChatGPT edition selects,
where it uses them, and the boundaries placed on their interpretation. Document
prose and organization are original to the edition; selecting a shared claim
does not transfer another provider's prose.

## Selection rationale and bounds

### Materials — PL-C001–PL-C009

Selected to exclude cellular/foam-core pipe, DWV fittings, mixed non-pressure
systems, pneumatic conversion, damaged material, and inadequately cured joints.

Boundaries:

- PL-C001–PL-C003 establish exclusions, not approval of any remaining design.
- PL-C004–PL-C007 identify factors that reduce or delay rated performance; they
  do not provide a combustion-service rating.
- PL-C008 is carried prominently: a pressure mark is a component screen, not
  certification of a homemade assembly for combustion or projectile use.
- PL-C009 governs the cure lock as **at least 48 hours, and longer whenever the
  actual manufacturer instructions or conditions require it**.

### Combustion and outdoor fire — PL-C010–PL-C014

Selected to explain bounded fuel-air combustion and to control unintended
ignition.

Boundaries:

- The edition teaches lean/rich limits without presenting a universal fuel
  dose, spray duration, pressure prediction, or performance-tuning recipe.
- A failed ignition is never “topped up”; the command card instead applies the
  fixed misfire hold and full venting rule.
- No selected claim is interpreted to permit liquid accelerants, oxygen
  enrichment, improvised fuel transfer, burn-ban operation, or dry-fire-risk
  operation.

### Flight, containment, and rules — PL-C015–PL-C019

Selected to distinguish the ideal ballistic model from safe site control and
to require location-specific legal checks.

Boundaries:

- PL-C015 is used only as a classroom no-drag model.
- PL-C016–PL-C017 prevent a computed range or landing point from being treated
  as containment. A walked empty range, positive earthen backstop, and margin
  for misses, fragmentation, drag, wind, and model error remain mandatory.
- PL-C018 is an example of a specific prohibition, not a complete statement of
  law. PL-C019 therefore requires checking state, local, landowner, and
  site-manager rules for the actual location.

### PPE, impulse noise, and adult control — PL-C020–PL-C025

Selected as governing constraints in all three documents.

Boundaries:

- Impact-rated eye protection with side protection and correctly fitted
  earplugs **plus** earmuffs are required for every person present.
- A phone or ordinary sound-level-meter reading cannot clear impulse-noise
  risk.
- One named sober adult controls the launcher, fuel, ignition, commands,
  misfires, and cease-fire. Minors do not fuel, ignite, clear a misfire, or
  handle a loaded launcher.
- Muzzle discipline, marked firing line, clear line of fire, and an untouched
  60-second downrange misfire hold are fixed controls.

## Claims deliberately not made

The shared library contains no evidence establishing:

- manufacturer approval of PVC for combustion-launcher service;
- a universally safe fuel, dose, pressure, projectile, angle, or range;
- a universally lawful place to build, possess, transport, or operate one;
- that one successful firing proves workmanship or future safety;
- that PPE can substitute for range containment, supervision, or elimination
  of a hazard.

The ChatGPT edition does not make or imply any of those claims.

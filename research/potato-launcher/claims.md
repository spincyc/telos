# Atomic claim library

## Materials

- **PL-C001** — Source says: PVC cellular-core pipe is not for pressure
  applications. [PL-S01]
- **PL-C002** — Source says: a PVC system using DWV fittings is not pressure
  rated even if some pipe is dual-marked. [PL-S01]
- **PL-C003** — Source says: the manufacturer prohibits PVC systems for
  compressed air or gases. [PL-S01]
- **PL-C004** — Source says: PVC working pressure must be derated as temperature
  rises. [PL-S02]
- **PL-C005** — Source says: sunlight exposure can reduce PVC impact strength.
  [PL-S02]
- **PL-C006** — Source says: solvent-weld cure depends on diameter, fit,
  temperature, humidity, cement, and service pressure. [PL-S03]
- **PL-C007** — Source says: colder, wetter, or higher-pressure conditions can
  require longer setup or cure. [PL-S03]
- **PL-C008** — Conservative inference: a printed pressure rating screens out
  clearly unsuitable DWV/cellular-core parts, but does not certify a home-built
  assembly for combustion pulses or launcher use. [PL-S01, PL-S02]
- **PL-C009** — Conservative inference: retain a 48-hour minimum cure gate and
  extend it whenever the cement manufacturer's chart or label requires longer.
  This is deliberately stricter than many warm-weather chart entries. [PL-S03]

## Combustion and fire

- **PL-C010** — Source says: a fuel-air mixture burns only between its lower and
  upper flammability limits. [PL-S04, PL-S05]
- **PL-C011** — Source says: too little fuel is too lean and too much is too rich
  to propagate flame; “more fuel” is not a monotonic route to combustion.
  [PL-S04]
- **PL-C012** — Source says: some flammable aerosols can exhibit flame
  projection or flashback. [PL-S04]
- **PL-C013** — Conservative inference: fuel must stay in its labeled original
  container, away from the firing point and all unintended ignition sources;
  never improvise with a liquid accelerant. [PL-S04, PL-S06]
- **PL-C014** — Conservative inference: do not operate during a burn ban, red
  flag warning, drought/dry-fuel conditions, or whenever the site manager
  forbids ignition; keep an appropriate extinguisher and water at hand.
  [PL-S06]

## Flight and legality

- **PL-C015** — Source says: in the no-drag model, horizontal motion is uniform
  while vertical motion accelerates downward at \(g\). [PL-S07]
- **PL-C016** — Source says: real atmospheric flight includes drag, so the
  no-drag equations are a model, not a guaranteed impact point. [PL-S07]
- **PL-C017** — Conservative inference: calculations cannot replace a walked,
  empty downrange area with a positive earthen backstop and a margin for misses,
  ricochet, fragmentation, and model error. [PL-S07]
- **PL-C018** — Source says: at least one NPS unit specifically prohibits potato
  launchers. [PL-S08]
- **PL-C019** — Conservative inference: confirm state, local, landowner, and
  site-manager rules before transport, assembly, or use. [PL-S08]

## Personal protection and supervision

- **PL-C020** — Source says: flying-object hazards call for eye protection with
  side protection meeting an incorporated Z87.1 standard or equivalent.
  [PL-S09]
- **PL-C021** — Source says: impulsive sound can cause immediate acoustic trauma
  and common meters can under-report peaks by clipping. [PL-S11]
- **PL-C022** — Source says: NIOSH recommends earmuffs over earplugs for impulse
  noise. [PL-S10]
- **PL-C023** — Conservative inference: every person present wears impact-rated
  eye protection and correctly fitted plugs plus muffs before the launcher is
  loaded or fueled. [PL-S09, PL-S10, PL-S11]
- **PL-C024** — Conservative inference: a single named sober adult controls the
  launcher, fuel, ignition, commands, misfires, and cease-fire; minors only
  observe or perform specifically assigned non-energetic tasks under direct
  supervision. [PL-S09, PL-S10]
- **PL-C025** — Conservative inference: muzzle discipline, a marked firing line,
  a clear line of fire, and a 60-second untouched downrange wait after a misfire
  are fixed administrative controls, not optional technique. [PL-S10, PL-S11]

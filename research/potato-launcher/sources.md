# Source catalog

All links were accessed 2026-07-26. Government and manufacturer publications
are preferred because the claims concern safety-critical behavior.

## Materials and joining

### PL-S01 — Charlotte Pipe, *Plastics Product Knowledge Guide*

- Publisher: Charlotte Pipe and Foundry Company
- URL: https://www.charlottepipe.com/uploads/documents/technical/BR-PK.pdf
- Type: manufacturer product guide
- Relevant evidence: PVC Schedule 40 DWV systems are non-pressure systems;
  cellular-core pipe is never for pressure applications; combining pipe with
  DWV fittings produces a system that is not pressure rated; PVC is not for
  compressed air or gases.

### PL-S02 — Charlotte Pipe, *Plastics Technical Manual*

- Publisher: Charlotte Pipe and Foundry Company
- URL: https://global.charlottepipe.com/TECHNICAL/TECHNICAL%20AND%20INSTALLATION%20DATA/CHARLOTTE_PLASTICS_TECH_MANUAL.PDF
- Type: manufacturer technical manual
- Relevant evidence: working pressure is temperature-dependent; sunlight can
  reduce PVC impact strength; pipe must not exceed its maximum working
  temperature or derated pressure.

### PL-S03 — Oatey, *Solvent Weld Cure Times*

- Publisher: Oatey Co.
- URL: https://www.oatey.com/sites/default/files/2020-11/01_SolventWeldCureTimes_CureChart_LCS358G.pdf
- Type: manufacturer cure-time chart
- Relevant evidence: cure time depends on pipe diameter, socket fit,
  temperature, humidity, cement, and operating pressure; cool conditions and
  higher pressure extend cure time; damp or humid weather requires 50 percent
  more handling/set time.

## Combustion and fire

### PL-S04 — OSHA, *Hazard Communication: Guidance for Hazard Determination*

- Publisher: U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration
- URL: https://www.osha.gov/hazcom/ghd053107
- Type: federal technical guidance
- Relevant evidence: combustion occurs only within a fuel's lower and upper
  flammability limits; below the lower limit a mixture is too lean, and above
  the upper limit it is too rich; aerosols may be classified by flame
  projection or flashback.

### PL-S05 — NIST, “Flammable Limit”

- Publisher: National Institute of Standards and Technology
- URL: https://www.nist.gov/glossary-term/24026
- Type: federal scientific glossary
- Relevant evidence: a flammable limit is an upper or lower fuel concentration
  in air, at specified temperature and pressure, that can be ignited.

### PL-S06 — U.S. Fire Administration, *Outdoor Fire Safety*

- Publisher: Federal Emergency Management Agency
- URL: https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/outdoor-fires/
- Type: federal public-safety guidance
- Relevant evidence: unintentional human actions lead outside fires; outdoor
  fire conditions require deliberate prevention and local awareness.

## Flight and site control

### PL-S07 — NASA Glenn Research Center, *Ballistic Flight Equations*

- Publisher: National Aeronautics and Space Administration
- URL: https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/ballistic-flight-equations/
- Type: federal educational physics reference
- Relevant evidence: neglecting drag, horizontal velocity is constant and
  vertical acceleration is \(-g\); position follows
  \(x=U_0t\), \(y=V_0t-gt^2/2\); aerodynamic drag makes real flight more
  complicated.

### PL-S08 — National Park Service, *Hunting — Lake Roosevelt NRA*

- Publisher: U.S. National Park Service
- URL: https://www.nps.gov/laro/planyourvisit/hunting.htm
- Type: federal site rule
- Relevant evidence: one federal recreation area expressly prohibits devices
  capable of launching projectiles, including potato launchers. This
  demonstrates that rules are site-specific and cannot be assumed.

## Eyes and hearing

### PL-S09 — OSHA, 29 CFR 1910.133, *Eye and Face Protection*

- Publisher: U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration
- URL: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.133
- Type: federal regulation
- Relevant evidence: hazards from flying particles require suitable eye/face
  protection and side protection; compliant protectors meet listed ANSI/ISEA
  Z87.1 editions or demonstrated equivalents.

### PL-S10 — NIOSH, *Provide Hearing Protection*

- Publisher: National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health
- URL: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/noise/prevent/ppe.html
- Type: federal occupational-health guidance
- Relevant evidence: engineering and administrative controls precede PPE;
  fit matters; NIOSH recommends double protection (earmuffs over plugs) for
  impulse sounds.

### PL-S11 — NIOSH, *How Can We Measure Impulse Noise Properly?*

- Publisher: National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health
- URL: https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/bulletin/2018/impulse-noise.html
- Type: federal research bulletin
- Relevant evidence: impulse noise can be more hazardous than continuous noise
  of equal energy; a high-intensity impulse can cause immediate acoustic trauma;
  ordinary sound meters may clip and miss the true peak.

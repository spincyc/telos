# Electricity & Magnetism — shared source register

Provider-neutral research for any Telos edition. Retrieval dates are recorded so
later authors can re-check a source rather than silently inheriting it.

## S01 — OSHA electrical safety standard

- **Publisher:** U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration
- **Title:** *29 CFR 1910.333 — Selection and use of work practices*
- **URL:** https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.333
- **Retrieved:** 2026-07-26
- **Authority:** Federal regulation; primary legal text for covered workplaces.
- **Use:** Deenergization, verification, qualified-person boundary, and the
  limited meaning of the 50 V threshold.
- **Caution:** An occupational rule is not a complete home-laboratory design
  standard. Its exception below 50 V is conditional; it does not declare every
  sub-50 V source safe.

## S03 — OSHA 50 V rulemaking rationale

- **Publisher:** U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration
- **Title:** *Electric Power Generation, Transmission, and Distribution;
  Electrical Protective Equipment* (final rule, 2014)
- **URL:** https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/federalregister/2014-04-11
- **Retrieved:** 2026-07-26
- **Authority:** Federal Register rulemaking record.
- **Use:** Why 50 V is treated as a recognized shock-hazard threshold.
- **Caution:** A threshold for required controls is not a promise that lower
  voltages cannot burn, ignite, or become hazardous through stored energy.

# Electricity full-edition atomic additions

| ID | Reusable claim | Support | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-FULL-001 | Capacitance is charge stored per potential difference, \(C=Q/V\). | EF01 | Ideal lumped model. |
| E-FULL-002 | Energy stored by an ideal capacitor is \(\tfrac12CV^2\). | EF01 | Real capacitors have voltage, polarity, ESR, and discharge limits. |
| E-FULL-003 | An inductor opposes change in current and stores magnetic-field energy. | EF02 | Qualitative learner treatment. |
| E-FULL-004 | Ideal transformer voltage ratio equals turns ratio. | EF02, EF03 | Losses and regulation make real ratios approximate. |
| E-FULL-005 | An ideal LC system exchanges energy between electric and magnetic fields; resistance damps it. | EF02, EF03 | No perpetual oscillation claim. |
| E-FULL-006 | Resonant response depends on frequency and damping. | EF02 | Learner investigation remains low-energy. |
| E-FULL-007 | Mutual inductive coupling depends on changing flux linking the second circuit. | EF02, EF03 | Geometry and core strongly affect coupling. |
| E-FULL-008 | Eddy currents oppose the flux change that produces them and dissipate energy as heat. | EF03 | Do not imply magnetic braking destroys energy. |
| E-FULL-009 | A loudspeaker converts varying electrical current to mechanical motion through magnetic force. | EF03 | Simplified dynamic-speaker model. |
| E-FULL-010 | A homopolar motor produces torque from current crossing a magnetic field. | EF03 | Direct short heats cell and wire; brief AA-cell operation only. |
| E-FULL-011 | Spark gaps conduct after gas breakdown and can generate broadband interference. | EF02 | Explanatory only; no learner spark construction. |
| E-FULL-012 | Small high-powered magnets present serious ingestion hazards. | EF06 | Keep inaccessible to young children; count before and after use. |

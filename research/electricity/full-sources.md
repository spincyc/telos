# Electricity full-edition research additions

Retrieved 2026-07-26. These supplement, rather than replace, the shared source
register.

## EF01 — DOE Electrical Science, Volume 1

- U.S. Department of Energy, *DOE Fundamentals Handbook: Electrical Science,
  Volume 1*, DOE-HDBK-1011/1-92.
- https://www.standards.doe.gov/standards-documents/1000/1011-BHdbk-1992-v1
- Use: electrostatics, capacitance, DC circuit fundamentals.

## EF02 — DOE Electrical Science, Volume 3

- U.S. Department of Energy, *Electrical Science, Volume 3*,
  DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92.
- https://www.standards.doe.gov/standards-documents/1000/1011-BHdbk-1992-v3
- Use: AC, inductance, capacitance, resonance, transformers.

## EF03 — Georgia State University HyperPhysics

- Department of Physics and Astronomy, Georgia State University,
  *Electricity and Magnetism* concept index.
- http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/emcon.html
- Use: university-maintained cross-check for Lorentz force, Faraday/Lenz law,
  transformer ratios, resonance, eddy currents, speaker and motor action.

## EF04 — University of Colorado PhET

- University of Colorado Boulder, PhET Interactive Simulations,
  *Circuit Construction Kit: DC* and teacher resources.
- https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/circuit-construction-kit-dc
- Use: research-based representational practice connecting schematics,
  measurement, and observable circuit behavior.

## EF05 — NOAA lightning safety

- National Weather Service, *Lightning Safety*.
- https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning
- Use: atmospheric discharge context and the instruction that no place outside
  is safe during thunderstorms. The Kelvin water dropper is never operated
  outdoors in thunderstorm conditions.

## EF06 — CPSC magnet ingestion warning

- U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, *Magnets Information Center*.
- https://www.cpsc.gov/Safety-Education/Safety-Education-Centers/Magnets
- Use: small high-powered magnet ingestion hazard for motor, induction, and
  eddy-current demonstrations.

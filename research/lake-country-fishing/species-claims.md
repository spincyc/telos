# Fish and cooking atomic claims

All claims were checked 2026-07-26. `Departure check` means the printed value
must yield to the live Wisconsin DNR page.

## Inventory

### LCF-FISH-001

Pine Lake's DNR record lists largemouth and smallmouth bass as common, and
panfish, northern pike, and walleye as present. **Source:** WI-DNR-PINE-FISH.

### LCF-FISH-002

North Lake's DNR record lists panfish, largemouth and smallmouth bass, and
northern pike as common, and walleye as present. **Source:** WI-DNR-NORTH-FISH.

### LCF-FISH-003

The broad current records do not identify individual panfish or claim cisco or
muskellunge abundance. Retaining Claude's eleven species is a publication-scope
decision, not a claim that all eleven have current surveyed populations.
**Basis:** LCF-FISH-001/002 and owner direction.

## Consumption and kitchen

### LCF-FOOD-002

The statewide inland baseline places bluegill, crappies, yellow perch,
sunfish, and rock bass in the panfish category: up to one serving/week for
women under 50 and children under 15, unrestricted for women 50+ and men.
Predators and unlisted species are one/month for the first group and one/week
for the second. Musky is do-not-eat for the first group and one/month for the
second. Water-specific advice may be stricter. **Sources:** WI-DNR-CHOOSE,
WI-DHS-EAT. **Volatility:** check the query before eating.

### LCF-FOOD-003

Wisconsin directs readers to compare water, species, and fish length with the
current advisory; smaller, younger fish are generally the better contaminant
choice. **Sources:** WI-DNR-CHOOSE, WI-DHS-EAT.

### LCF-FOOD-004

Fish should reach 145°F internally or be opaque and separate easily.
**Source:** USDA-SAFE-TEMP.

### LCF-FOOD-005

Wild freshwater fish may carry parasites; ordinary pickling or an improvised
freezing step is not presented as a validated safety process in these guides.
**Source:** UMN-FISH-PRESERVE. **Status:** conservative editorial application.

### LCF-FOOD-005A

Immediate accurate percussive stunning followed by a killing step such as
brain destruction is preferable to leaving a retained fish to die by
asphyxiation. **Source:** MD-DNR-HUMANE-DISPATCH. **Status:** general method
applied across the retained freshwater species; an inaccurate strike must be
repeated immediately.

## Diagram and method status

### LCF-FOOD-006

Each cooking guide contains an original monochrome dispatch sequence, cleaning
map, and species-labeled bone map. Northern pike and muskellunge use a distinct
Y-bone field and channel-removal method. These are instructional editorial
syntheses, not copied source illustrations.

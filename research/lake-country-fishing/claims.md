# Atomic claim ledger

Status is `accepted` unless stated otherwise. “Inference” identifies editorial
reasoning rather than a statement made verbatim by a source.

## Lake identity and character

### LCF-LAKE-001

Pine Lake is Waukesha County waterbody WBIC 779200: 711 acres, maximum depth
85 ft, mean depth 39 ft, and a spring lake. Its recorded bottom composition is
90% gravel, 5% sand, and 5% muck.
**Source:** WI-DNR-PINE-FACTS. **Volatility:** Low.

### LCF-LAKE-002

The Pine Lake record lists one boat landing and multiple local boating
ordinances.
**Source:** WI-DNR-PINE-FACTS. **Volatility:** Medium; departure check.

### LCF-LAKE-003

North Lake is Waukesha County waterbody WBIC 850800: 440 acres, maximum depth
78.4 ft, and a drainage lake. Its recorded bottom composition is 50% sand, 30%
gravel, and 20% muck.
**Source:** WI-DNR-NORTH-FACTS. **Volatility:** Low.

### LCF-LAKE-004

The North Lake record lists local boating ordinances, including slow-no-wake
areas/hours and high-water provisions.
**Source:** WI-DNR-NORTH-FACTS. **Volatility:** High; departure check.

### LCF-LAKE-005

An angler should treat Pine as a steep, deep, hard-bottom spring-lake problem
and North as a smaller drainage-lake problem, then confirm the day’s fish
location with observation or electronics.
**Basis:** Inference from LCF-LAKE-001 and LCF-LAKE-003. **Volatility:** Low.

## Law and stewardship

### LCF-RULE-001

Wisconsin DNR directs anglers to check season dates for both the species and
the particular water because not all waters follow general dates.
**Sources:** WI-DNR-REGS, WI-DNR-SEASONS-2026. **Volatility:** High; departure
check.

### LCF-RULE-002

A waterbody daily bag limit and the total daily bag limit across waters are
different constraints; neither authorizes exceeding the other.
**Source:** WI-DNR-BAG. **Volatility:** High; departure check.

### LCF-RULE-003

Before transport, boaters and anglers must inspect equipment, remove attached
aquatic plants or animals, and drain water; they may not move live fish away
from a waterbody and should put unwanted bait in the trash.
**Source:** WI-DNR-AIS. **Volatility:** Medium; departure check.

### LCF-FOOD-001

Wisconsin’s statewide inland fish-consumption advice is a baseline, but some
waters have additional advice; the reader must compare water, species, and
fish length with current advice.
**Source:** WI-DNR-CONSUMPTION. **Volatility:** High; departure check.

## On-water safety

### LCF-SAFE-001

Every vessel, including canoes, kayaks, and paddleboards, must carry a
USCG-approved wearable life jacket for every person; vessels 16 ft or longer
must also carry an immediately accessible approved throwable device. Wearing
the fitted jacket is safer than merely carrying it.
**Source:** WI-DNR-PFD. **Volatility:** Medium; departure check.

### LCF-SAFE-002

Cold water can rapidly remove the ability to self-rescue. Wisconsin DNR advises
wearing a life jacket, dressing for conditions, and planning conservatively.
**Source:** WI-DNR-BOAT-SAFETY. **Volatility:** Low.

### LCF-SAFE-003

A pre-launch routine should include checking weather, boat capacity, lights
and safety equipment; leaving alcohol ashore; and telling someone the route
and return time.
**Source:** WI-DNR-BOAT-SAFETY. **Volatility:** Low.

### LCF-SAFE-004

If thunderstorms are possible, the safest choice is not to launch. If thunder
or threatening weather develops, stop fishing and head to port or safe shelter
early; a small open boat is not safe shelter.
**Sources:** NOAA-NWS-INLAND, NOAA-NWS-THUNDER. **Volatility:** Low.

## Fish care

### LCF-CARE-001

Catch-and-release harm is reduced by planning before the fish arrives, using
tackle that limits deep hooking, minimizing handling and air exposure, and
returning the fish once it can maintain balance.
**Source:** WI-DNR-RELEASE. **Volatility:** Low.

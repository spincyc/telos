# Fish and kitchen source register

Checked 2026-07-26. These records supplement the project-wide source register.
The ChatGPT fish and cooking editions use the sources below independently; no
Claude prose or finished diagram is a source.

## Wisconsin government

### WI-DNR-PINE-FISH

- **Title:** Pine Lake — Fish (WBIC 779200)
- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **URL:** https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/lakes/lakepages/LakeDetail.aspx?page=fish&wbic=779200
- **Use:** Current broad fish inventory: largemouth bass and smallmouth bass
  common; panfish, northern pike, and walleye present

### WI-DNR-NORTH-FISH

- **Title:** North Lake — Fish (WBIC 850800)
- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **URL:** https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/lakes/lakepages/LakeDetail.aspx?page=fish&wbic=850800
- **Use:** Current broad fish inventory: panfish, largemouth bass, smallmouth
  bass, and northern pike common; walleye present

### WI-DNR-PINE-RULES / WI-DNR-NORTH-RULES

- **Title:** Lake Regulation Details, WBIC 779200 / 850800
- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **URLs:** https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/fisheriesmanagement/Public/LakeRegulation/Details?WBIC=779200
  and https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/fisheriesmanagement/Public/LakeRegulation/Details?WBIC=850800
- **Use:** 2026–27 water-specific season, minimum length, and daily bag rules
- **Volatility:** High; check before each trip

### WI-DNR-SPECIES

- **Title:** Wisconsin's Fishes; Hook Your Catch
- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **URLs:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/species and
  https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/outreach/hookyourcatch.html
- **Use:** Identification, habitat, feeding, and seasonal biology

### WI-DNR-CHOOSE / WI-DHS-EAT

- **Titles:** Eating Your Catch — Making Healthy Choices; Eat Fish, Safely:
  Recommendations
- **Publishers:** Wisconsin DNR; Wisconsin Department of Health Services
- **URLs:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/consumption and
  https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/water/fish/recommendations.htm
- **Use:** Statewide baseline, susceptible-population categories, serving
  concept, contaminant context, and water/length query requirement
- **Volatility:** High; query exact water and species before eating

## Food safety and extension

### USDA-SAFE-TEMP

- **Title:** Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart
- **Publisher:** FoodSafety.gov / U.S. Department of Agriculture
- **URL:** https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures
- **Use:** Fish: 145°F internal temperature or opaque and separating easily

### UMN-FISH-PRESERVE

- **Title:** Preserving Food at Home: Meat, Fish and Poultry
- **Publisher:** University of Minnesota Extension
- **URL:** https://extension.umn.edu/preserve-your-own-food/meat-fish-and-poultry
- **Use:** Freshwater-fish parasite caution and preservation boundaries

### MD-DNR-HUMANE-DISPATCH

- **Title:** Northern Snakehead Control and Management Plan
- **Publisher:** Maryland Department of Natural Resources
- **URL:** https://dnr.maryland.gov/fisheries/Documents/fmp/ChesapeakeBay_NorthernSnakehead_FMP_3-2023.pdf
- **Use:** Government fish-welfare guidance identifying immediate percussive
  stunning or brain spiking as humane dispatch methods; the edition applies
  the general method, not the plan's snakehead control policy

## Editorial scope decision

The eleven Claude-covered species are retained: black crappie, bluegill,
cisco, largemouth bass, muskellunge, northern pike, pumpkinseed, rock bass,
smallmouth bass, walleye, and yellow perch. The current DNR lake pages name no
additional species more specifically than “panfish.” Therefore no additional
species sheet is asserted from those broad records. Cisco and muskellunge are
retained because the owner requires full Claude taxonomic coverage; their
sheets explicitly avoid claiming current abundance from the broad lake pages.

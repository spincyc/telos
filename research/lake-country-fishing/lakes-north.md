# North Lake evidence record

Checked 2026-07-26. Waterbody identity is fixed by WBIC, not by name.

## Identity and physical character

- **Water:** North Lake, Waukesha County, WBIC 850800.
- **DNR facts:** 440 acres; 78.4 ft maximum; drainage lake; bottom recorded as
  50% sand, 30% gravel, 20% muck; eutrophic.
- **DNR contour source:** Wisconsin Conservation Department sheet compiled in
  1898 and revised 1941. It reports 449 acres, 3.44 shoreline miles and 78.4 ft
  maximum. It is historical and marked not for navigation.
- **Current shoreline check:** 2024 Wisconsin statewide NAIP, 0.6 m aerial
  orthophotography, was consulted for shoreline, roads and access context.
  NAIP is USDA/FSA public-domain imagery. No imagery is reproduced.

## Species evidence for publication filtering

The current DNR lake record explicitly names **panfish, largemouth bass,
smallmouth bass, northern pike and walleye**. These are the defensible current
compendium inclusions. The exact-water regulation page is not an inventory and
explicitly warns that its categories may not reflect fish actually present.
Do not add muskellunge, cisco, whitefish, catfish, trout, sturgeon or invasive
gobies on the strength of the regulation table alone.

The DNR record does not resolve “panfish” to species. Bluegill, black crappie,
pumpkinseed, yellow perch and rock bass must therefore be labeled as candidate
members of that broad group unless a survey-level record is added.

## 2026--27 hook-and-line rules

DNR lookup for WBIC 850800, checked 2026-07-26:

- motor trolling: up to three hooks, baits or lures per angler;
- bass: catch-and-release year-round; harvest May 2, 2026--March 7, 2027,
  14 in minimum, five daily;
- northern pike: May 2, 2026--March 7, 2027, 26 in minimum, two daily;
- walleye/sauger/hybrids: May 2, 2026--March 7, 2027, 18 in minimum, three daily;
- panfish: open all year, no minimum, 25 daily;
- rock/yellow/white bass: open all year, no minimum, unlimited;
- cisco/whitefish: open all year, no minimum, ten daily;
- muskellunge/hybrids: May 2--December 31, 2026 on open water, 40 in minimum,
  one daily.

Categories do not establish presence. Re-run the exact-water lookup and inspect
launch signage before every trip.

## Access and ordinances

Unlike Pine, the current DNR facts page does not list a numbered public boat
landing. The historical sheet labels a road at the north end, but that is not
proof of present public launching rights. The map marks a **verify-access**
point rather than promising a launch.

The DNR record lists local ordinances including slow-no-wake hours and areas,
speed limits, swimming rules and high-water emergency restrictions. DNR warns
that the database can lag; posted signage controls the practical departure
check.

## Map interpretation

The line map was independently drafted from the 1898/1941 source, checked
against 2024 NAIP for present shoreline context. Numbered areas are
structure-based starting hypotheses: north flat; north-east break; central
channel edge; west-lobe rim; west-lobe basin; central saddle; southeast hump;
south basin edge; south neck. Confirm depth and vegetation with lawful
electronics and observation. Not for navigation.

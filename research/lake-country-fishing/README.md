# Lake Country Fishing — shared research

Provider-neutral research for publications about Pine Lake and North Lake in
Waukesha County, Wisconsin. An AI edition may reuse these claims, reject them,
or add better evidence; it should record its choices in a selection manifest.

## Contract

- `sources.md` describes authoritative witnesses and when they were checked.
- `claims.md` contains atomic claims with source IDs, scope, and volatility.
- Provider manifests identify the claims actually used and explain editorial
  choices. They are not substitutes for the underlying evidence.
- A claim marked **departure check** must never be presented as durable. Link
  the reader to the live authority and tell them to check immediately before
  fishing.
- Lake maps and remembered fishing spots are hypotheses, not navigation data.

The first contributor is the ChatGPT edition, researched 2026-07-26. Future
providers should append rather than silently alter a claim. When evidence
changes, deprecate the old claim and add a new ID.

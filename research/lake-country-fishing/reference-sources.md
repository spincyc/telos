# Fieldcraft and tackle source register

Checked 2026-07-26. This topical register extends `sources.md` for the full
ChatGPT fishing library.

## WI-DNR-HLT

- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **Title:** Hook, Line & Thinker — Field Knowledge
- **URL:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/anglereducation/HLT.html
- **Use:** tackle, knot, casting, safety, ethics, and beginner instruction
- **Authority:** official state angler-education curriculum

## WI-DNR-HOOK-CATCH

- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **Title:** Hook your catch with these tips
- **URL:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/outreach/hookyourcatch.html
- **Use:** species-specific tackle, bait, depth, and seasonal starting points
- **Limit:** introductory guidance; local observations still control

## WI-DNR-RELEASE-FULL

- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **Title:** Catch-and-release guidelines
- **URL:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/RecordFish_CatchReleaseGuidelines.pdf
- **Use:** tackle choice, fight duration, landing, wet handling, air exposure,
  deep hooking, barotrauma, and revival
- **Authority:** official state guidance synthesizing published research

## WI-DNR-ICE

- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **Title:** Ice safety
- **URL:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/Education/OutdoorSkills/IceSafety
- **Use:** variability of ice strength, travel planning, safety equipment,
  current hazards, and self-rescue
- **Volatility:** conditions are always local and current; no static thickness
  declares ice safe

## WI-DNR-ICE-WALLEYE

- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **Title:** Fishing walleye through the ice
- **URL:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/Icefish_WEIceFishTips.pdf
- **Use:** tip-up configuration, hook choice, depth marking, and eating-size
  framing
- **Limit:** expert field note, not regulation

## WI-DNR-DINNER

- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **Title:** Fishing for Dinner
- **URL:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/AnglerEd_FishingForDinnerOnlineGuide.pdf
- **Use:** humane dispatch, field preservation, bait disposal, cleaning,
  cooking, contaminants, and beginner-course design

## WI-DNR-2026

- **Publisher:** Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources
- **Title:** New Regulations for the 2026–2027 Fishing Season
- **URL:** https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/Regulations/NewRegulations
- **Use:** dated statewide changes and the requirement to resolve water-specific
  regulations
- **Volatility:** high; verify before departure

## UMN-HARD-WATER

- **Publisher:** University of Minnesota Extension
- **Title:** Hard Water Anglers Ice Fishing Guide
- **URL:** https://extension.umn.edu/yd-curriculum/hard-water-anglers-ice-fishing-guide
- **Use:** youth-facing ice tackle, lure, bait, stewardship, and safety
- **Authority:** university-extension curriculum

# Shared field-reference evidence record

Checked 2026-07-26. The six reference sheets select Wisconsin DNR's Hook, Line
& Thinker, Hook Your Catch, catch-and-release, ice-safety, Fishing for Dinner,
boat/bait-law and 2026--27 regulation materials. They are independent editorial
syntheses, not rewrites of the Claude sheets.

Core evidence boundaries:

- location advice is a starting hypothesis; observation and fish response
  control adjustment;
- use tackle strong enough to shorten fights; prepare a knotless rubber net,
  wet hands and tools before landing;
- minimize air exposure, support large fish horizontally, and cut line rather
  than prolong deep-hook removal when appropriate;
- no static thickness makes ice safe; current, narrows, inlets and outlets add
  risk;
- inspect, remove and drain before transport; do not move live fish away from
  water; dispose of unwanted bait in trash;
- exact-water regulations, consumption guidance and local signs must be
  checked before departure.

Sources and URLs are registered in `reference-sources.md`.

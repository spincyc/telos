# Fieldcraft and tackle atomic claims

These claims supplement the project-wide ledger. IDs are stable and may be
selected independently.

| ID | Atomic reusable claim | Support | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| LCF-FIELD-001 | Tackle instruction should connect each component to a purpose, then require the learner to assemble and test it. | WI-DNR-HLT | Pedagogical application is editorial. |
| LCF-FIELD-002 | A first trip should minimize idle rigging time by arriving with simple tackle prepared and a spare usable setup. | WI-DNR-HLT | Editorial synthesis from beginner instruction. |
| LCF-FIELD-003 | Species location and presentation advice is a starting hypothesis; depth, weather, forage, and observed fish response determine the day’s adjustment. | WI-DNR-HOOK-CATCH | Never present a tactic as guaranteed. |
| LCF-CARE-002 | Use tackle strong enough to retrieve a fish promptly; extended fights increase exhaustion. | WI-DNR-RELEASE-FULL | Match tackle to target and conditions. |
| LCF-CARE-003 | A knotless soft-rubber net, wet hands, horizontal support for large fish, minimal air exposure, and prepared tools reduce handling injury. | WI-DNR-RELEASE-FULL | Species-specific hazards still apply. |
| LCF-CARE-004 | If a fish is deeply hooked, cutting the line promptly can be less damaging than prolonged extraction. | WI-DNR-RELEASE-FULL | Do not use stainless hooks when a hook may be left. |
| LCF-CARE-005 | Anglers intending release should consider capture depth; artificial swim-bladder deflation is not recommended in the DNR guidance. | WI-DNR-RELEASE-FULL | Coldwater/deep-water response is species-specific. |
| LCF-ICE-001 | No single thickness, appearance, age, or temperature makes ice completely safe; strength varies within one waterbody. | WI-DNR-ICE | Current local assessment is mandatory. |
| LCF-ICE-002 | Avoid inlets, outlets, narrows, and other current areas; carry picks, flotation, rope, and a waterproofed phone, travel with others, and leave a plan. | WI-DNR-ICE | Equipment does not make unsafe ice safe. |
| LCF-ICE-003 | A tip-up can suspend live bait near bottom while allowing a fish to run; a depth marker makes the set repeatable. | WI-DNR-ICE-WALLEYE | Confirm current line and bait regulations. |
| LCF-BAIT-001 | Live bait and water transport rules are disease- and invasive-species controls, not optional tackle preferences. | WI-DNR-DINNER, WI-DNR-AIS | Departure check. |
| LCF-RULE-004 | The 2026–27 guide contains statewide and water-specific changes; the exact water and species must be resolved before fishing. | WI-DNR-2026 | High volatility; departure check. |
| LCF-PED-001 | Youth-facing ice instruction should integrate tackle, bait, stewardship, and safety rather than treating safety as a detachable preface. | UMN-HARD-WATER | Editorial application. |

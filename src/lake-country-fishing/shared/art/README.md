# Shared graphite textures

## `water-graphite.png`

- Purpose: observational water texture clipped inside exact deterministic lake
  polygons. It carries no geographic or bathymetric facts.
- Method: generated with the built-in image-generation workflow on 2026-07-27.
- Prompt: subtle top-down lake water in fine monochrome graphite on white
  paper; faint horizontal wind ripples and tonal grain; no shoreline, land,
  objects, labels, symbols, borders, or dark areas.
- Source evidence: none. Shoreline geometry comes from each provider map's
  dated aerial-imagery review; bathymetry comes from its cited survey.
- Review rule: keep the texture faint enough that contours, soundings, access
  status, scale, and field marks remain unambiguous in grayscale.

The texture may be shared because it contains no finished map geometry or
provider-specific editorial conclusion. Each finished map retains independent
shoreline geometry, bathymetric reconstruction, labels, and composition.

\newcommand{\CompendiumQuestions}{%

verified fish contact?

confusion species were excluded?

current authoritative source, rather than a regulation or recollection?

\begin{telospanel}{How to use it}
The numbered spots on the depth map are the guide's spine. Every species sheet
and most rig sheets refer back to them, so Pine 4'' or North 5'' always
means the same piece of water. Read the lake sheet first; after that the guide
is meant to be flipped through rather than read.
\end{telospanel}

\section*{Field-learning loop}
Use the binder as a route through evidence, not as proof that every named fish
is present or every mapped spot will produce. Read in this order:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Lake sheet:} locate the numbered spot, contour, access context,
dated rules, and limits of the map.
\item \textbf{Species sheet:} identify a catch from diagnostic structure before
assigning a name or interpreting abundance.
\item \textbf{Rig and reference sheets:} choose a controlled presentation and
record the variables that can change.
\item \textbf{Cooking sheet:} use only after lawful retention, positive
identification, and a current consumption-advice check.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Post-trip proof}

observed, how it was identified, and what remains uncertain.

\begin{teloshazard}{A field note does not replace the evidence filter}
A photograph or catch can support identification of that individual. It does
not by itself establish abundance, persistence, a fishable population, or the
status of another species. Preserve every sheet's printed qualification until
a current authoritative record changes it.
\end{teloshazard}

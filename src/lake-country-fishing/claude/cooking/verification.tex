\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.23\linewidth}LL@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Ask now} & \textbf{Pass condition} & \textbf{If it fails}\\
\midrule
Is the fish still cold? & Firm, clean-smelling flesh at or below
\qty{40}{^\circ F}. & If time above \qty{40}{^\circ F} is uncertain, discard;
do not try to ``cook safety back in.''\\
Did the knife enter gut or gall? & No green stain, fecal material, or sour
odor on edible flesh. & Cut away a generous contaminated margin; wash hands,
knife, and board before touching clean meat.\\
Are the portions actually boneless? & Bright light plus two slow fingertip
passes, head-to-tail and tail-to-head. & Return the portion to the clean board;
trim or recut. Never rely on batter or sauce to hide a bone.\\
Is the center done? & A clean instant-read probe enters sideways into the
thickest piece and reads \qty{145}{^\circ F}. & Keep cooking in short
increments; clean the probe before rechecking.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{telospanel}{Final proof}
Set one finished portion on a clean plate, split its thickest point, and confirm
opaque, separating flakes with no translucent core. Record the time and
temperature once when learning a new pan, oven, or portion thickness. Cool
leftovers shallowly and refrigerate within two hours. The proof is the measured
center, not a timer, crust color, or confident guess.
\end{telospanel}

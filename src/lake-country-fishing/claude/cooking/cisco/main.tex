\section{Cleaning it}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Bleed it at the boat} --- a cut through the gill arches --- and put it straight on ice. This matters more for cisco than for anything else here.
\item \textbf{Scale it} if you are smoking or grilling skin-on. Cisco scales are large and lift with almost no effort.
\item \textbf{Gut it}, and for smoking leave the head on --- it gives you something to hang it by.
\item \textbf{Or fillet normally:} behind the gill to the backbone, along the spine to the tail, ribs out. The flesh is soft, so use a sharp blade and few strokes.
\item \textbf{Leave the belly meat on.} It is the fattiest and best part, and it is what makes smoked cisco worth doing.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Bones and what to watch for}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.20]
\telosfact{Pin bones}{A row of fine pin bones runs down the fillet. Pull them with needle-nose pliers, in the direction they lean, before cooking.}
\telosfact{Rib cage}{Fine and soft.}
\telosfact{Smoked whole}{The frame lifts out in one piece after smoking --- another reason to smoke rather than fillet.}
\telosfact{Soft flesh}{Handle gently and do not stack.}
\end{telosfacts}

\section{Cooking it}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.18\linewidth}L@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{How}\\
\midrule
Smoked & The right answer. Brine six hours in salt, brown sugar and water. Air-dry until tacky --- that skin, the pellicle, is what smoke sticks to. Cold-smoke over apple or alder at \qty{180}{^\circ F} for three to four hours. Outstanding, and it keeps.\\
Grilled whole & Scaled, gutted, scored, oiled and salted. Hot grill, four minutes a side. The oil bastes it from inside.\\
Pan-fried & Fillets, skin on, skin-side down in a hot dry pan --- there is enough fat in the fish that it needs almost no oil. Two minutes, flip, one minute.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{telospanel}{The one fish here that rewards a smoker}
Every other fish in this guide is lean, and lean fish smoke poorly --- they go dry and stringy. Cisco is the exception, and a lake that holds them is unusual in this part of Wisconsin. If you have ever wanted a reason to build a smoker, a November cisco from Pine Lake is it. \sheetref{Cisco}
\end{telospanel}

\begin{eatingfacts}
\telosfact{Verdict}{The best-eating fish in either lake, and arguably in fresh water. Sweeter and firmer than walleye, in a smaller package. \telosrating{5}}
\telosfact{Texture}{Firm, fine-grained, faintly sweet. Never mushy.}
\telosfact{Best size}{9\,in and up makes filleting worth the effort. Below that, pan-dress instead.}
\telosfact{Yield}{Low --- about 25\%, and a 9\,in perch gives two fillets of barely 1.5\,oz. This is a fish you need a dozen of.}
\telosfact{Ice fishing}{Winter perch out of \qty{34}{^\circ F} water are the best of all. \sheetref{Seasonal Calendar}}
\telosfact{Scales}{Fine and very well attached. Filleting and skinning beats scaling every time.}

\section{Filleting a small fish}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Use a \textbf{thin, very sharp, flexible blade}. A thick knife on a 9\,in perch wastes half the fish.
\item \textbf{Cut behind the gill plate} down to the backbone.
\item \textbf{Run the blade along the backbone} to the tail in one stroke. On a fish this small, resist sawing.
\item \textbf{Skin it} from the tail, and take the ribs with the skin if you can --- on perch it is often faster to cut the rib section away entirely and accept the small loss.
\item \textbf{Or pan-dress instead:} scale, gut, cut the head off behind the gills, and cook it whole on the bone. Below about 9\,in this gives you more food than filleting does.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Bones and what to watch for}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.20]
\telosfact{Spiny dorsal}{The first dorsal fin is genuinely sharp and so are the gill covers. Handle a live perch from the head-on direction, or with a cloth.}
\telosfact{Rib cage}{Small and fine. Cut it out or pull it with pliers.}
\telosfact{Nothing else}{No Y-bones, no pin bones. A perch fillet is clean.}
\telosfact{Pan-dressed}{Eaten off the bone, the frame lifts out in one piece after cooking.}
\end{telosfacts}

\section{Cooking it}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.18\linewidth}L@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{How}\\
\midrule
Pan-fried & The only method that really matters. Egg wash, then cracker crumbs or fine cornmeal. Butter, medium-high, ninety seconds a side. They are small --- they cook fast.\\
Perch fry & The Friday institution. Beer batter, \qty{375}{^\circ F}, two to three minutes. Serve with rye bread, coleslaw and a lemon wedge, which is how it is done here.\\
Whole and crisp & Pan-dressed fish, scored, dusted in seasoned flour, shallow-fried until the fins go crisp. Eat the fins. They are the best part and nobody believes it until they try.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{telospanel}{Worth the work}
Perch are small and cleaning a dozen takes a while, which is exactly why so few people bother and why the ones who do eat better than everybody else. Set up an assembly line: one person fillets, one skins, one rinses and ices. Twenty minutes for a family meal of the best fish in the county.
\end{telospanel}

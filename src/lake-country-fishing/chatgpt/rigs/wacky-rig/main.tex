\RigSheet{Wacky rig}{Lets both ends of a straight worm pulse on a slow fall.}
{{main line}{small hook}{O-ring / center}{worm ends}}
{Tie a small hook and place it through an O-ring at the worm midpoint, or
through the plastic when necessary. Start weightless.}
{Cast past the target, allow a slack-controlled fall, lift once or twice, and
repeat. Most takes occur on the fall.}
{Use over clear shelf edges with long casts.}
{Use around shallow breaks and sparse cover.}
{Add minimal weight only when depth or wind prevents control.}
{Use line and tackle capable of steering bass away from cover promptly.}

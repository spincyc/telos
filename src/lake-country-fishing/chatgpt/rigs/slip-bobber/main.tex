\RigSheet{Slip bobber}{Suspends bait at a measured depth while remaining castable.}
{{stop + bead}{sliding float}{weight}{hook + bait}}
{Thread stop, bead and float; add only enough weight to stand the float; tie a
small hook. Set the stop to the target depth and test beside the boat.}
{Cast beyond the target, reel into position, and watch for lift, travel or
submergence. Reel tight before a smooth hook-set.}
{Probe the outside vegetation edge and island saddle.}
{Cover panfish water on the north flat and lobe rims.}
{Sound actual depth, mark the stop setting, then move it in 1 ft steps while
holding weight, hook, and bait constant.}
{Keep loose hooks secured; a float is not a substitute for watching the line.}

\RigSheet{Three-way rig}{Separates bait and sinker on two branches for controlled depth.}
{{main line}{three-way swivel}{short sinker drop}{long bait leader}}
{Tie main line to one swivel eye, a sacrificial sinker drop to the second, and
a longer leader with hook or lure to the third. Test for tangles.}
{Move slowly along a contour with sinker contact intermittent, not plowing.
Keep leader clear during turns.}
{Trace long deep basin edges.}
{Follow the eastern wall and central corridor.}
{Shorten both branches in weeds or current.}
{Multiple branches tangle easily; transport assembled rigs in a keeper.}

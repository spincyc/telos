\RigSheet{Split-shot rig}{Presents a small bait naturally with minimal hardware.}
{{main line}{pinched shot}{leader space}{hook + bait}}
{Tie the hook first. Pinch one removable shot 12--24 in above it without
damaging the line; add weight only when wind or depth demands it.}
{Cast gently, let it settle, then crawl or swim it with pauses while keeping
enough contact to detect a change.}
{Use on gradual west shelves and quiet pockets.}
{Use over shallow flats and the central saddle.}
{Increase leader space for softer fall; shorten it for control.}
{Wear eye protection when pinching or removing shot; discard damaged line.}

\RigSheet{Lindy rig}{Separates a walking sinker from a slowly moving bait.}
{{sliding sinker}{swivel}{leader}{hook + bait}}
{Slide sinker onto main line, tie swivel, then add a 3--6 ft leader and hook.
Use a leader that keeps the bait clear without defeating control.}
{Drift or troll slowly enough to feel bottom. Feed brief slack after a pickup,
then reel tight; avoid prolonged delay that encourages deep hooking.}
{Follow long 20--40 ft basin edges.}
{Trace the main-basin wall and west-lobe rim.}
{Shorten the leader in weeds; lengthen over clean hard bottom.}
{Watch other lines and traffic; motor trolling limits must be rechecked.}

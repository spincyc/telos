\RigSheet{Carolina rig}{Carries a soft plastic along bottom behind a sliding weight.}
{{sinker + bead}{swivel}{leader}{hook + plastic}}
{Slide sinker and bead onto main line; tie swivel; add leader and an
offset hook. Rig the plastic straight so it tracks without spinning.}
{Cast across a contour, settle, then sweep with the rod and recover slack.
Pause on hard-bottom changes.}
{Search broad gravel shelves and tapers.}
{Search the north flat and saddle approaches.}
{Compare \qty{1/4}{oz} and \qty{1/2}{oz} at the same depth; record sink time
and the lightest weight that keeps bottom contact.}
{Do not snap a loaded sinker toward people or fragile equipment.}

\RigSheet{Quick-strike rig}{Supports a large legal live bait for an immediate predator hook-set.}
{{strong main line}{float + leader}{two-hook harness}{legal bait}}
{Use predator-rated line, leader and a purpose-built two-hook harness sized to
the bait and target. Balance the float; keep points exposed and separated.}
{Attend continuously. On a take, remove slack and set promptly rather than
waiting for swallowing. Land, unhook and release or dispatch efficiently.}
{Test weed edges only where current species rules allow.}
{Test lobe rims and vegetation only where lawful.}
{Measure bait depth from the lower hook; balance the smallest float that holds
the bait, then change depth in 2 ft steps---never hook-set delay.}
{Two hooks and large fish create severe puncture risk. Use long pliers, cutters,
gloves and a large rubber net; never hand-land.}

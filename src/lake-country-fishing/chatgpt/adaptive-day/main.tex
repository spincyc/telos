\begin{teloshazard}{Weather is the first pattern}
Do not launch when thunderstorms are possible. At thunder or threatening
weather, stop fishing and head to port or safe shelter early. An open boat is
not safe shelter. Wear a fitted life jacket; cold water removes self-rescue
ability quickly.
\end{teloshazard}

\section{A compact pattern ladder}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Visible shallow cover.} Quiet approach; compact bait; parallel
the edge before crossing it.
\item \textbf{First break outside cover.} Use a presentation that maintains
depth and transmits bottom.
\item \textbf{Hard/soft or depth transition.} Search along it; slow only after
contact, marks, or a bite.
\item \textbf{Suspended evidence.} Match the marked depth; do not fish beneath
fish merely because bottom is easy to see.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{telospanel}{The one-change rule}
If location is unknown, search. If fish are seen but do not respond, change
presentation. If they bite but do not stay pinned, change hook, size, or
handling. Never change location, depth, speed, and bait together: success then
teaches nothing.
\end{telospanel}

\section{Release is decided before the cast}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{itemize}
\item Use tackle strong enough to land the fish promptly.
\item Prefer tackle that reduces deep hooking.
\item Ready net, pliers, measure, and camera first.
\item Wet hands; support the fish; do not squeeze.
\item Minimize handling and air exposure.
\item Return it when it can hold balance.
\end{itemize}

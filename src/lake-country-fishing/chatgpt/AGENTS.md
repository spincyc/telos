# ChatGPT fishing edition requirements

These requirements are the owner’s publication contract. Future agents must
read this file before changing anything below this directory.

## Scope

- Reproduce the complete *kind* of library present in the Claude edition:
  lake sheets, lake-specific compendia, fish sheets, rig sheets, cooking
  guides, lures and bait, seasonal calendar, knots, handling and release,
  reading water, and first-trip guidance.
- Research and write the ChatGPT edition independently. Do not copy Claude
  prose, conclusions, finished maps, or finished diagrams.
- Retain every fish and rig covered by Claude. Add species or rigs when
  authoritative current research shows they are materially relevant to Pine
  Lake or North Lake.
- A lake compendium includes only the species and tactics relevant to that
  lake, plus the shared reference material needed to use it.
- Replace the former three-card ChatGPT set; it is not part of the intended
  edition.

## Research

- Use provider-neutral records under `research/lake-country-fishing/`.
- Prefer government primary sources. Authoritative university-extension and
  Sea Grant sources are allowed for biology, handling, and food preparation.
- Print the dated 2026–27 regulations and always instruct the reader to check
  the live Wisconsin DNR rules for the exact water, species, and date.
- Build lake maps independently from original DNR bathymetric material.
  Current public satellite imagery may be used to improve shoreline and access
  research, but the printed result remains a clean line map. Do not reuse
  Claude’s finished map.

## Cooking guides

Every harvested-species guide must cover:

1. humane dispatch and safe handling;
2. cleaning and scaling or skinning as appropriate;
3. species-specific filleting;
4. species-specific bone layout and deboning;
5. preparation before heat;
6. at least two suitable cooking methods;
7. current consumption guidance; and
8. the distinction between legal harvest size and preferred eating size.

Muskellunge receives legal-harvest preparation rather than release-only
treatment. Page count is unrestricted. Use ample original, species-specific
instructional diagrams, including distinct structures such as pike Y-bones.

## Visual and print contract

- Every Telos document is black and white. Do not rely on color for meaning,
  hierarchy, warnings, maps, keys, or diagrams.
- Use original monochrome line diagrams rather than photographs for
  instructional sequences.
- US Letter is the default paper size. A one-page claim is binding only when a
  document explicitly makes it; cooking guides are expected to span pages.
- Check legibility in an actual grayscale raster preview before publication.

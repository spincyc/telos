\newcommand{\Debone}{Pull the firm pin bones in their forward angle or V-cut the front-center line. Inspect the shoulder and any cheek pieces independently.}
\newcommand{\TrimNotes}{Remove rib membrane, belly fat, and dark lateral tissue. Trimmed fat can reduce some fat-soluble contaminants but not mercury.}
\newcommand{\Prep}{Separate thick shoulder from tail; pat dry. Reserve equal portions for the same pan so none overcook.}
\newcommand{\CookOne}{Classic shallow-fried walleye}\newcommand{\CookOneText}{Apply a thin dry coating; fry in clean oil, turn once, and verify \qty{145}{^\circ F} in the thickest shoulder.}
\newcommand{\CookTwo}{Mustard-roasted walleye}\newcommand{\CookTwoText}{Brush with mustard and fine crumbs, roast on a preheated sheet, and remove when the center reaches \qty{145}{^\circ F}.}

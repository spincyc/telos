\begin{teloshazard}{Static paper is not the law}
Immediately before fishing, use Wisconsin DNR's current searchable regulations
for the exact water and species. Check season, length and bag limits, method,
license, and local posted boating rules. A total daily bag limit and a
waterbody daily limit are separate constraints.
\end{teloshazard}

\section{Before leaving home}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{gate}{Legal}
\begin{itemize}
\item Current license for each required angler
\item Pine WBIC 779200 or North WBIC 850800 selected
\item Water- and species-specific rules checked today
\item Current fish-consumption advice checked if harvesting
\item Launch and local ordinances checked
\end{itemize}
\end{gate}
\columnbreak
\begin{gate}{Conditions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Forecast, radar, wind, air and water temperature
\item Cancel for thunderstorm risk
\item Route and return time left with a shore contact
\item Crew, load, and plan within capacity plate
\item Fuel/charge and daylight margin
\end{itemize}
\end{gate}
\end{multicols}

\section{At the boat}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.25\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Wear} & Fitted, serviceable, USCG-approved life jacket for each person.
Carrying one cannot help if it is unreachable after a fall.\\
\textbf{Carry} & Wearable PFD for every person; immediately accessible
throwable device on vessels 16 ft or longer; communication, whistle, first aid,
lights, and safety gear appropriate to the boat.\\
\textbf{Control} & Sober operator; balanced load; no passenger on gunwale or
seatback; slow-no-wake and posted rules observed.\\
\textbf{Escape} & Know the nearest safe landing from every part of the route.
At thunder or threatening weather, leave early.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{At the ramp after fishing}
\begin{telospanel}{Inspect · Remove · Drain · Never move live fish}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Inspect boat, trailer, anchor, lines, net, waders, and fishing gear.
\item Remove attached plants, animals, and mud before highway transport.
\item Drain boat, motor, bilge, livewell, buckets, and fishing equipment.
\item Never move live fish away from the waterbody. Put unwanted bait in trash;
follow the current DNR exceptions for dealer minnows.
\end{enumerate}
\end{telospanel}

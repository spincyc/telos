\section*{What is bound}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.25\linewidth}L@{}}
\toprule
I. Lake & Independent bathymetric line map, access context, evidence limits,
and dated rules for \LakeName.\\
II. Fish + kitchen & Confirmed fish first, then a clearly divided candidate
panfish module; each field sheet is followed by its cooking guide.\\
III. Rigs & Eleven retained rig sheets, each useful for at least one supported
fish group or seasonal presentation on this water.\\
IV. Reference & Reading water, lures and bait, calendar, knots, handling and
release, and first-trip workflow.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

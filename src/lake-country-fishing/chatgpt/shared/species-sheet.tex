\begin{telospanel}{Intermediate questions}
Before looking at the species name, answer aloud: \emph{Where does the closed
jaw end? Are dorsal sections joined or separate? What is the tail-lobe shape?
Which mark is structural enough to survive poor light?} If two answers conflict,
record ``unresolved'' and do not force the identification.
\end{telospanel}

\begin{telospanel}{Keep only a fish you can use}
Decide before the cast. Bring ice, stop fishing when the planned meal is
secured, and use the species-specific cooking guide for dispatch, bone layout,
and safe preparation. Large old predators are valuable spawners and generally
carry more mercury than smaller fish.

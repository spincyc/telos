# Graphite portrait contract

These files are aesthetic identification portraits for the ChatGPT fishing
edition. They are original AI-assisted illustrations, not copied DNR plates.

- One adult fish, exact lateral profile, head left, entire fish visible.
- Fine graphite natural-history treatment on an opaque white background.
- Grayscale only; no labels, scenery, shadows, signatures, or watermarks.
- Species-specific body, jaw, fin, tail, and marking anatomy must survive
  ordinary black-and-white printing.
- Keep sufficient master resolution for at least 300 effective pixels per inch
  at the size used in `shared/species-sheet.tex`.
- Validate the complete unlabeled set and every likely confusion pair against
  authoritative species references before publication.

Do not use these portraits as filleting, cut-path, or bone diagrams. The
provider-owned vector anatomy under `shared/fish-art/` remains the controlled
instructional layer for cooking sheets.

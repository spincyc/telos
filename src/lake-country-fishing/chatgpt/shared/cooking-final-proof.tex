\section{Prove the meal is ready}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.25\linewidth}Lp{.20\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Gate} & \textbf{Evidence to record} & \textbf{If it fails}\\
\midrule
Cold and clean & Fish stayed at or below \qty{40}{^\circ F}; viscera came out
intact; board and knife were cleaned before cooked food returned. &
Discard contaminated flesh; clean and restart.\\
Bone free & Fingertip sweep in both directions; split the thickest finished
piece and inspect under bright light. & Return the portion to the board and
re-trim; do not hide uncertainty under coating.\\
Even portions & Thickest and thinnest pieces measured: \rule{9mm}{.4pt} and
\rule{9mm}{.4pt} in. & Separate batches or adjust timing; do not average them.\\
Safe heat & Lowest stable reading in the geometric center:
\rule{12mm}{.4pt}\({}^\circ\)F; target at least \qty{145}{^\circ F}. &
Continue cooking and measure again in a new probe track.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{teloscard}{Questions before serving}
Where is the thickest portion, and why is the probe there? What observation
would reveal a hidden pin bone? If one thin piece finished early, what control
keeps it from drying while the thick piece reaches temperature? Which time,
temperature, and advisory facts belong in the meal record?
\end{teloscard}

\noindent\textbf{Final proof.} Write one sentence each for the claim
(``ready to serve''), the direct evidence, and why that evidence supports the
claim. Record refrigeration time for leftovers: \rule{22mm}{.4pt}.

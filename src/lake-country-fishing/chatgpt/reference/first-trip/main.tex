\section{Before leaving}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Confirm legal access, weather, exact-water 2026--27 rules, consumption
advice and posted boating ordinances. Leave a float plan.
\item Pack fitted PFDs, water, sun/weather layers, charged waterproofed phone,
first aid, pliers, cutters, knotless rubber net and AIS-cleaning supplies.
\item Rig one slip bobber and one simple jig or split-shot setup; carry a spare.
Use legal bait from a lawful source.
\end{enumerate}
\section{A two-hour search}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.2\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule
First 15 min & Observe wind, clarity, temperature, vegetation, traffic and bait.\\
Next 45 min & Fish one shallow edge. Change depth before changing everything.\\
Next 45 min & Test one break or saddle. Record bottom and contact depth.\\
Last 15 min & Photograph notes, clean/drain/remove, secure hooks and leave on time.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\section{Beginner rule}
One safe, repeatable presentation fished attentively teaches more than a box of
constant changes. A bite is evidence; no bite is also evidence. Move only after
you can state what depth, cover and speed you actually tested.
\begin{teloshazard}{Stop conditions}
Return early for thunder, rising wind, poor visibility, equipment trouble,
cold stress, unsafe traffic or uncertainty about access or law. A small open
boat is not lightning shelter. Wear the PFD.
\end{teloshazard}

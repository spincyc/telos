\begin{teloshazard}{The trip begins with a live check}
Confirm legal access, weather, exact-water 2026--27 rules, consumption advice,
and posted boating ordinances. Leave a float plan. Return early for thunder,
rising wind, poor visibility, equipment trouble, cold stress, unsafe traffic,
or uncertainty about access or law. Wear the fitted PFD.

box? Can I reach cutters while seated? Can my partner state the return plan?
If any answer is no, fix it on shore.

\section{The one-variable ladder}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Depth:} move the bobber stop 1 ft; make 5 attentive casts.
\item \textbf{Speed:} keep depth fixed; compare still, slow pull, then pause.
\item \textbf{Position:} shift one cast length along the same edge.
\item \textbf{Presentation:} change lure only after you can name the water
column and cover already tested.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Intermediate question:} Was “no bite” a complete test, or did the lure
foul, miss depth, or spend most of the retrieve away from the feature?

\section{Troubleshooting}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{.23\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule Symptom&Corrective check\\\midrule
Bobber lies flat&Weight may be on bottom or missing. Shorten depth until it
stands, then lower in measured steps.\\
Constant weed fouling&Confirm hook is clean every cast; move 1 ft shallower or
parallel the outside edge.\\
Cannot repeat contact&Mark boat position and casting direction; reduce wind
drift before changing lure.\\
Too many decisions&Return to one rig, one edge, and the next 1-ft depth step.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\begin{telospanel}{Trip complete when}
You returned on time, left no plants or line, recorded at least one verified
depth/cover observation, and can name the first test for the next visit. A
catch is useful evidence, not the only measure of success.
\end{telospanel}

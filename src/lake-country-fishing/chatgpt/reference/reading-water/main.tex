\begin{teloshazard}{Map discipline}
Historical contours are generalized and not for navigation. Watch traffic,
weather, depth, and hazards directly. Never stare at electronics while
underway.

\section{Read in layers}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{.17\linewidth}p{.29\linewidth}p{.46\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule\textbf{Signal}&\textbf{Possible meaning}&\textbf{Test, not conclusion}\\\midrule
Windward edge&Food or warmer surface water may collect.&Make one controlled
pass, then compare the sheltered side.\\
Point / inside turn&Travel route between depths.&Cast across and along; record
depth of every contact.\\
Vegetation edge&Cover plus oxygen/light boundary.&Find today's actual inside,
top, and outside edges; old maps cannot show them.\\
Hard/soft change&Different prey or spawning habitat.&Use bottom feel or lawful
electronics; mark where texture changes twice.\\
Bait on electronics&Forage is present at that moment.&Fish its depth, including
suspended water; leave if predators never appear.\\
Temperature / clarity&Visibility, metabolism, and plant depth differ.&Adjust
distance, speed, size, and depth one variable at a time.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Three passes, one comparison}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Map pass.} Travel safely and slowly. Record surface temperature,
depth at weed top, outside edge, bottom transition, bait height, and wind angle.
\item \textbf{Control pass.} Hold lure, depth, speed, and line angle constant
through the best feature. Mark every bottom/weed touch and bite.
\item \textbf{Change pass.} Alter one variable: 1 ft shallower/deeper, one speed
step, or along-feature instead of across-feature.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Field measurements}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{.22\linewidth}p{.32\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule Measure&Method&Verification\\\midrule
Contact depth&Known stop length, jig bottom count, or electronics&Same depth
appears on two passes.\\
Edge position&Waypoint plus boat depth and casting bearing&Lure finds the edge
again from a new approach.\\
Bottom change&Compare sharp taps, dragging, and plant debris&Texture change
persists over several feet, not one rock.\\
Bait height&Read top and bottom of the school&Presentation intersects that
band, not merely the boat depth.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Troubleshooting the inference}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{.25\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule Problem&Correction\\\midrule
Waypoint produces nothing&Re-find depth, cover, and wind angle; a coordinate is
not the feature.\\
Electronics show “fish”&Check arch consistency, boat speed, and repeated
returns; then fish the marked depth as a test.\\
Bottom feels inconsistent&Shorten cast, use enough weight, and compare in known
hard and soft areas.\\
Wind prevents repetition&Use a shorter drift, controlled angle, or sheltered
comparison. Safety outranks completing the pass.\\
Too many signals&Rank them: safe access, depth, cover, bait, then presentation.
Write one sentence predicting the next contact.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\begin{telospanel}{A feature is verified when}
You can relocate both of its boundaries, place the lure in the intended band,
and reproduce contact from a recorded position. Fish presence remains a
time-specific observation, never a permanent property of the waypoint.
\end{telospanel}

\section{Measurements to carry forward}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{.24\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule Observation&Record so it can be compared\\\midrule
Surface temperature&Instrument, location, time, sun/wind, and sheltered versus
windward difference; avoid false precision.\\
Plant stage&First green shoots, top depth, outside-edge depth, density, and
whether hooks return clean.\\
Water column&Temperature/oxygen evidence if available; depth of bait and fish;
never infer oxygen from depth alone.\\
Turnover candidate&Near-uniform temperature, mixed clarity/odor, dispersed
bait, and changed bite together—not the calendar alone.\\
Ice condition&Direct local observations along the whole route; thickness at
one hole never certifies an area.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Stage checks and first tests}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{.2\linewidth}p{.35\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule Stage&Intermediate questions&Controlled first test\\\midrule
Warming shallows&Is it warmer on two readings? Is new cover present?&Compare
one protected edge with adjacent main-lake water at equal depth.\\
Developed weeds&Where are inside, top, and outside edges today?&Fish 1 ft above
the top, then the outside wall.\\
Layered summer&Is bait suspended? Is deep release responsible?&Hold at bait
height; change target/location if fish show stress.\\
Cooling / turnover&Are temperatures actually converging? Has clarity changed?&
Search multiple depths before slowing on a single feature.\\
Cold / ice&Can the route and return be made safely?&If uncertain, do not enter;
fish open water only with cold-water boating protection.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Troubleshooting calendar errors}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{“They should be shallow.”} Measure temperature and cover; compare
the next available depth rather than repeating the story.
\item \textbf{“Turnover killed the bite.”} Seek multiple matching indicators;
wind, turbidity, or presentation failure may look similar.
\item \textbf{“The weeds are gone.”} Check deeper, shorter, and greener growth;
surface dieback does not map the full plant bed.
\item \textbf{“Ice was safe yesterday.”} Conditions and routes change. Reassess
locally every trip; uncertainty is a stop condition.
\end{itemize}
\begin{telospanel}{Hypothesis passes when}
Two or more observable indicators agree, the predicted feature can be located,
and a controlled presentation can be repeated there. A catch strengthens the
case; a calendar label never substitutes for evidence.
\end{telospanel}

Improved clinch & Pass through eye; make 5--7 wraps; return through the first
loop, then the large loop; moisten and draw tight. General hook/lure knot.\\
Palomar & Double line through eye; tie a loose overhand; pass loop over tackle;
moisten and seat without crossing strands. Strong when the doubled loop fits.\\
Uni knot & Pass through eye; form a loop alongside standing line; wrap tag
through loop 5--7 times; moisten, close, and slide to eye. Versatile connection.\\
Surgeon's loop & Double the line; make an overhand loop, pass doubled end
through twice; moisten and seat evenly. Useful for loop-to-loop leaders.\\

\section{Every time}
Leave enough tag to finish correctly. Wet monofilament/fluorocarbon before
seating. Pull standing line and tag firmly, inspect for crossing, flattening or
heat damage, trim safely, then test harder than the expected presentation load.
Retie after abrasion, snags, tooth contact or unexplained failure.

\begin{teloshazard}{Hooks under tension}
Point hooks away from faces and hands. Wear eye protection when testing or
pulling free. Cut snagged line under control; never snap it toward the boat.
\end{teloshazard}

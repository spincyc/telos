\section{Before casting}
Match tackle to the largest plausible fish. Open pliers and cutters; wet and
stage a knotless rubber net; decide whether the fish can be released beside the
boat. Favor tackle that limits deep hooking when release is likely.

\section{At the fish}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Keep it in water when possible. Wet hands; never squeeze gills or eyes.
\item Support a large fish horizontally with two hands. Minimize air exposure;
photograph only when tools and camera are already ready.
\item Back out an accessible hook. If deep extraction will prolong trauma,
cut the line promptly; avoid stainless hooks where one may be left.
\item Return the fish when it maintains balance. Face it into gentle current
only when present; do not pump it violently.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{teloshazard}{Depth and temperature}
Deep capture and warm water increase risk. Wisconsin DNR does not recommend
artificial swim-bladder deflation. Change location, depth, tackle or target
rather than repeatedly producing fish unlikely to survive.
\end{teloshazard}

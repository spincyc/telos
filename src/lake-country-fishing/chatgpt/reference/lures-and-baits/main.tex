\begin{teloshazard}{Bait is regulated}
Inspect, remove, and drain before transport. Do not move live fish away from a
waterbody. Put unwanted bait in trash and obey current source, water, and
transport rules. Secure every exposed hook in storage.
\end{teloshazard}

\section{Start with the job, not the brand}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{.17\linewidth}p{.22\linewidth}p{.31\linewidth}p{.22\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule\textbf{Tool}&\textbf{Job}&\textbf{Start here}&\textbf{First adjustment}\\\midrule
Small jig&Depth contact; compact prey&Breaks, saddle, panfish or walleye
search&Change weight to restore feel.\\
Slip bobber + legal bait&Hold exact depth&Vegetation edges and flats&Move stop
in 1-ft steps.\\
Straight soft worm&Slow bass fall&Sparse cover and clear shelf edges&Weightless,
then minimally weighted.\\
Weedless plastic&Enter vegetation&West Pine shelf; North flats&Add only enough
weight to penetrate.\\
Crankbait&Cover a depth band&Hard edges with safe clearance&Running depth first,
then speed.\\
Spinner / spoon&Flash and vibration&Weed lanes and active pike water&Add a
bite-resistant leader when warranted.\\
Live minnow&Natural movement&Jig, float, or lawful predator rig&Keep lively;
verify source and transport law.\\
Leech / crawler&Slow natural bait&Float or bottom rig&Downsize hook and use only
what can be presented responsibly.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Tune one presentation}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Verify action beside the boat.} The lure runs upright; soft
plastic is straight; bait can move; hook point is clear.
\item \textbf{Establish depth.} Count down a jig, measure the bobber stop, or
confirm bottom contact. Record the number.
\item \textbf{Make five comparable casts.} Same lane, distance, speed, and
pause. Note weed, bottom, bite, or clean water.
\item \textbf{Change one variable.} Depth by 1 ft, jig weight one step, or
retrieve from steady to stop-and-go. Repeat.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Measurements that transfer}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{.24\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule Record&How to verify\\\midrule
Float depth&Measure stop to hook; subtract the suspended weight/hook drop if
precision matters.\\
Jig fall&Count seconds to first bottom touch in known depth; repeat with taut
and semi-slack line.\\
Crank clearance&Rod tip and retrieve keep periodic bottom/weed ticks without
continuous fouling.\\
Weed penetration&Weight enters the canopy yet comes back with the hook point
clear often enough to fish efficiently.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Troubleshooting}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{.24\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule Symptom&Corrective sequence\\\midrule
Lure spins&Check bent shaft, crooked plastic, fouled hook, and excessive speed
beside the boat before casting again.\\
No bottom feel&Use thinner line or one weight step heavier; shorten cast; verify
in known depth.\\
Every cast fouls&Inspect the actual weed-top depth; fish 1 ft above it, parallel
the edge, or switch to a weedless geometry.\\
Live bait dies quickly&Correct temperature, oxygen, crowding, and handling;
discard lawfully rather than moving it to another water.\\
One short strike&Inspect and sharpen/replace hook; do not instantly change
three variables. Repeat the verified lane first.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\begin{telospanel}{Presentation passes when}
It enters the intended depth band, remains correctly rigged, returns free of
hidden fouling, and can be repeated within the same cast lane. Only then does a
bite or no-bite compare meaningfully.
\end{telospanel}

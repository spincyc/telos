\begin{teloshazard}{Bait is regulated}
Inspect, remove, and drain before transport. Do not move live fish away from a
waterbody. Put unwanted bait in trash and obey current source, water, and
transport rules. Secure every exposed hook in storage.
\end{teloshazard}

\section{Live bait begins with a legal chain}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Before buying or collecting, verify.} Check the current Wisconsin
DNR bait rules for the source, species, waterbody, transport route, season, and
whether unused bait or water may leave the site. Keep the receipt or other
required source evidence with the bait.
\item \textbf{At transfer, identify.} Reject unknown fish, mixed accidental
species, visibly diseased bait, and containers with plants, mud, snails, or
other hitchhikers. ``Minnow'' is not an identification.
\item \textbf{At the launch, separate water from permission.} Water carried
from a dealer, home, or another waterbody can be regulated differently from
the bait itself. Recheck before adding, exchanging, draining, or transporting
any water.
\item \textbf{At departure, decide before pouring.} Never release unused bait,
plants, animals, or container water into the lake or on the shoreline. Follow
the current lawful disposal and transport route; when uncertain, seal it and
ask rather than improvise.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teloshazard}{No live-fish assumption survives the trip}
A bait that was lawful to possess may not be lawful to move away from a water,
reuse elsewhere, or transport in water. The live DNR rule and posted direction
beat this printed guide.
\end{teloshazard}

\subsection{Six-second bait verification}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Wet the holding hand; touch bait briefly and gently.
\item Confirm species/source and that the chosen bait is alive enough to
perform the intended job.
\item Match hook wire and gap to bait size; expose the point.
\item Pull beside the boat: upright, moving, not spinning, not fouled.
\item After every miss, weed contact, or hard cast, inspect position and injury.
\item Stop using bait that cannot be presented humanely, effectively, and
lawfully; dispose of it by the verified route.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Tune one presentation}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Verify action beside the boat.} The lure runs upright; soft
plastic is straight; bait can move; hook point is clear.
\item \textbf{Establish depth.} Count down a jig, measure the bobber stop, or
confirm bottom contact. Record the number.
\item \textbf{Make five comparable casts.} Same lane, distance, speed, and
pause. Note weed, bottom, bite, or clean water.
\item \textbf{Change one variable.} Depth by 1 ft, jig weight one step, or
retrieve from steady to stop-and-go. Repeat.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Troubleshooting}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{.24\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule Symptom&Corrective sequence\\\midrule
Lure spins&Check bent shaft, crooked plastic, fouled hook, and excessive speed
beside the boat before casting again.\\
No bottom feel&Use thinner line or one weight step heavier; shorten cast; verify
in known depth.\\
Every cast fouls&Inspect the actual weed-top depth; fish 1 ft above it, parallel
the edge, or switch to a weedless geometry.\\
Live bait dies quickly&Correct temperature, oxygen, crowding, and handling;
verify aeration and actual water temperature; discard lawfully rather than
moving it or its water to another waterbody.\\
One short strike&Inspect and sharpen/replace hook; do not instantly change
three variables. Repeat the verified lane first.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\begin{telospanel}{Presentation passes when}
It enters the intended depth band, remains correctly rigged, returns free of
hidden fouling, and can be repeated within the same cast lane. Only then does a
bite or no-bite compare meaningfully.
\end{telospanel}

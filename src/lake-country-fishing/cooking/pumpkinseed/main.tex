\begin{eatingfacts}
\telosfact{Verdict}{Indistinguishable from bluegill on the plate --- sweet, white, delicate. \telosrating{5}}
\telosfact{Size}{Usually smaller than the bluegill it shares a bed with, which is the only practical difference. A 7\,in pumpkinseed is a good one.}
\telosfact{Yield}{About 20\%. Below 7\,in, pan-dress rather than fillet.}
\telosfact{Mixed catch}{You will almost always be cleaning these alongside bluegill. There is no reason to separate them.}
\telosfact{Hybrids}{Bluegill and pumpkinseed interbreed freely. If a fish fits neither description, it eats the same either way.}
\telosfact{Freezing}{In water, not dry. Delicate flesh burns fast.}
\end{eatingfacts}

\section{Filleting or pan-dressing}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Shear off the spiny dorsal} with kitchen scissors before anything else. It makes the fish safe to hold.
\item \textbf{Cut behind the gill plate}, following the gill cover's curve, down to the backbone.
\item \textbf{Run the blade along the backbone} to the tail. The backbone sits high in a deep-bodied fish --- aim higher than instinct suggests.
\item \textbf{Lift the ribs out} with a shallow arc.
\item \textbf{Skin from the tail.}
\item \textbf{Under 7\,in, pan-dress instead:} scale against the grain with a knife back, gut, head off behind the gills, fins trimmed. Cook whole on the bone. More food and less work.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Bones and what to watch for}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.20]
\telosfact{Spines}{Sharp dorsal and anal spines. Shear them first.}
\telosfact{Rib cage}{Fine, easily removed.}
\telosfact{No pin bones}{Clean fillet.}
\telosfact{Whole fish}{The skeleton lifts out in one piece after cooking, which is the trick to show a child.}
\end{telosfacts}

\begin{telospanel}{Worth one lesson}
Before you clean a June catch, sort the pile into bluegill and pumpkinseed by the ear flap --- solid black versus an orange-red spot --- and count each. It takes two minutes, it teaches an identification that sticks, and it tells you something real about which fish that bay is producing. \sheetref{Pumpkinseed}
\end{telospanel}

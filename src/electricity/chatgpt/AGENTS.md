# ChatGPT electricity edition requirements

- Maintain a complete parallel to the Claude publication taxonomy: overview,
  safety, Lessons 01--10, build-the-coil, and every demonstration.
- Research and write independently. Provider editions may share the neutral
  research library, but must not copy another provider's prose or finished art.
- All publications and diagrams are black and white. Diagrams must remain
  intelligible on an ordinary monochrome printer.
- Preserve three distinct roles: a learner may perform only the explicitly
  bounded battery investigations; an adult facilitator controls materials and
  stop decisions; only an electrically qualified person may perform work for
  which qualification is required. Adult status never implies qualification.
- Learner work is limited to isolated battery sources at 12 V DC or less.
  Mains, opened appliances, large battery packs, charged power capacitors,
  ignition coils, sparks, and exposed high voltage are outside learner work.
- High-voltage and spark-gap material is explanatory or qualified-person-only.
  Never turn historical apparatus into a learner construction procedure.
- Every lesson should include a prediction, observable or measured evidence,
  an explanation, a controlled extension, and original diagrams.
- Use authoritative government, standards-laboratory, university, or
  peer-reviewed sources. Record reusable claims and sources in the neutral
  research library.

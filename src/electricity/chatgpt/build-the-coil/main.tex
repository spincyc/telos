\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item Mount components so no bare lead can bridge the battery. Provide strain relief and a clearly labeled master switch.\item Connect the primary through the pushbutton. Connect the secondary only to its meter or correctly resistor-limited LED.\item Adult performs continuity, resistance, polarity, meter-jack, and short-path inspection while deenergized.\item Pulse for less than one second; record receiver response. Compare coil gap, alignment, turns, and core one at a time.\item Disconnect the battery for every adjustment. Stop on warmth or unexpected behavior.\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{This instrument combines current-produced field, changing flux, mutual induction, and geometry. It deliberately does not make sparks and is not a Tesla coil. Continuous DC gives little secondary response after the switching transient. A measured low-energy pulse provides more useful evidence than an uncontrolled high-voltage spectacle.}

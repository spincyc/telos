\begin{telospanel}{Question}
How can the direction and relative strength of an electric interaction be
recorded when we cannot see the field itself?
\end{telospanel}

\section{Build a repeatable detector}
\textbf{Materials:} clear tape, two drinking straws, thread, index card, wool
or clean dry hair. No outlet-powered device is used.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Hang a 12 cm tape strip from thread as a light “paddle”; fold the last
centimetre into a handle. Make a second identical strip.
\item Press both strips to the same clean tabletop, label the handles A and B,
then pull them up by the handles. Bring B toward hanging A without touching.
\item Make a third strip C. Press A and C together face-to-back, peel them apart,
and compare A--C with A--B. Record attraction or repulsion, distance, and motion.
\item Repeat three times. Humidity can weaken the effect; “no motion” is data.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teloshazard}{Keep scale honest}
This static-tape investigation is not permission to experiment with outlets,
CRT displays, ignition coils, stun devices, Van de Graaff terminals, or storms.
Those sources differ radically in available energy and voltage. \stopline
\end{teloshazard}

On an index card, mark detector positions 1, 2, 4 and 8 cm from a charged strip.
At each position, release the detector from the same angle and rate the initial
motion 0--3. Repeat, then draw arrows. State what your map directly records and
what it infers.

\section{The charge scale}
Charge comes in opposite signs; like signs repel and unlike signs attract.
Macroscopic charge is assembled from elementary charges whose magnitude is
\[
e=1.602\,176\,634\times10^{-19}\ {\rm C}\quad\hbox{(exact in SI).}
\]
The experiment identifies relative sign; it does not tell whether the moving
charge carriers were positive or negative.

\begin{teloscard}{Extension: test a model}
Predict what doubling distance will do to your motion rating. Make five trials
at each distance. Does the evidence support “same,” “steadily weaker,” or an
exact inverse-square law? Explain why a tape paddle is too crude for the last
claim.
\end{teloscard}

\begin{teloshazard}{Learner domain}
Only isolated, current-limited battery circuits at \textbf{12 V DC or less},
using the parts and limits printed in the investigation. Disconnect before
rewiring. This conservative course rule controls exposure; it does not mean
everything below 12 V is harmless.
\end{teloshazard}

\section{Green domain — learner builds, adult checks}
\begin{itemize}
\item AA-cell holders or the specifically listed small battery source
\item intact insulated clip leads, listed resistors, LEDs, small coils
\item hand-moved magnets, compass, passive sensors
\item resistance/continuity with every source removed
\item voltage/current measurement only exactly as the investigation specifies
\end{itemize}

\section{Black-line domain — stop; outside this course}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.22]
\telosfact{Household power}{No outlet experiments, socket probes, mains wiring,
power strips, lamps, appliances, extension-cord repair, or opened power supplies.}
\telosfact{High voltage}{No Tesla coils, ignition coils, microwave parts, flyback
transformers, stun devices, spark gaps, CRTs, neon transformers, or arcs.}
\telosfact{Stored/fault energy}{No large capacitor banks, vehicle/lead-acid
batteries, lithium packs without an approved protected holder, or improvised
high-current supplies. Low voltage can still start a fire or cause burns.}
\telosfact{Unknown apparatus}{No unlabelled adapter, salvaged supply, damaged
cell, frayed lead, swollen battery, or mystery component.}
\end{telosfacts}

\begin{telospanel}{Adult ownership is not qualification}
The facilitator owns the kit, checks the planned circuit, and makes every stop
decision. That role does not qualify anyone to work on exposed mains or high
voltage. OSHA confines exposed energized-parts work to qualified persons and
normally requires exposed parts to be deenergized and verified with test
equipment. This course contains no procedure for that work.
\end{telospanel}

\section{Stop signals}
Disconnect without touching a hot or damaged part. Tell the adult.
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{itemize}
\item warmth, odor, smoke, sparking
\item swelling, leak, discoloration
\item damaged insulation or loose metal
\item meter over-range or unstable display
\item circuit differs from the diagram
\item someone proposes “just try it”
\end{itemize}
\end{multicols}

\begin{gate}{Five checks before connect}
[\ ] Listed battery source only\quad
[\ ] No direct source short\quad
[\ ] Meter mode, range and jacks correct\quad
[\ ] Adult checked diagram against build\quad
[\ ] Everyone knows the disconnect point
\end{gate}

\begin{teloshazard}{Electrical emergency}
Do not touch a person who may still be energized. Disconnect power without
entering the current path and call emergency services. Do not improvise rescue
or rely on a switch/indicator as proof that hazardous equipment is deenergized.
\end{teloshazard}

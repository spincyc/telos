\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item Adult counts and mounts the magnet on the negative cell end. Bend balanced wire to touch the positive button and barely brush the magnet edge.\item Clear the area; adult makes contact for no more than five seconds while learner observes direction.\item Disconnect, check wire and cell temperature, and allow cooling.\item Flip the magnet and repeat once. Store the magnet securely.\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{Current crossing the magnet's field experiences force; the geometry turns that force into torque. Reversing either current or field reverses rotation; reversing both preserves it. This is nearly a direct short, so most input can become heat. That is why the trial is brief, adult-controlled, AA alkaline only, and never lithium.}
\def\extension{Predict four combinations of cell direction and magnet direction before testing only the minimum needed to check the pattern.}

\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item \textbf{Orientation null:} pulse identically while rotating the receiver in 15-degree steps. Record LED response or meter peak and find the minimum.\item \textbf{Field portrait:} remove the receiver; use the compass to map primary field direction at grid points during brief, consistent current pulses.\item Overlay both maps. Ask whether the weak coupling orientation matches little linked flux.\item Power off and disconnect before every repositioning.\end{enumerate}}

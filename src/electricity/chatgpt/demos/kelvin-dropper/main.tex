\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item Adult checks stable supports, dry floor, spill tray, and generous ring separation. Operate indoors only, never during thunderstorm conditions.\item Start two smooth drips and watch the electroscope connected by the apparatus design.\item Stop flow well before any spark, snapping, hair raising, or painful sensation. Do not approach conductors while operating.\item With water stopped, adult discharges through the intended high-resistance path and verifies zero indication before touch.\end{enumerate}}

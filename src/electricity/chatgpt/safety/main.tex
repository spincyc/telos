\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item Work dry, uncluttered, and away from jewelry, loose metal, drinks, and damaged cells.
\item Assemble with power disconnected. Adult checks source, polarity, meter jacks, bare conductors, and likely short paths.
\item Energize only for the stated interval. One hand owns the switch. Disconnect before moving a lead.
\item Stop immediately for heat, swelling, leakage, odor, smoke, pain, damaged insulation, or behavior not predicted.
\item Count small magnets before and after; store them inaccessible to children. Never use a lithium cell in a direct-short demonstration.\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{Voltage alone is not a safety verdict. Current path and duration affect shock; available current and stored energy affect heating, arcs, and fire. OSHA's qualified-person distinction is a useful boundary even though this is not a workplace course. The course therefore excludes mains and exposed high voltage rather than teaching a learner to manage them.}
\def\extension{Run a no-power rehearsal. The learner points to the disconnect, names every stop condition, and demonstrates where each meter lead goes. If the rehearsal is uncertain, do not energize.}

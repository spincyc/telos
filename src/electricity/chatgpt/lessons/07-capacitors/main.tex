\def\procedure{\small\begin{enumerate}\item Adult confirms capacitance, voltage rating, and polarity. Begin discharged through the resistor.\item Connect battery--resistor--capacitor. Measure voltage across the capacitor every five seconds.\item Disconnect the battery and discharge only through the resistor while recording voltage.\item Repeat with a second resistance; never short the leads with metal.\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{\small Capacitance is \(C=Q/V\). The resistor limits current, so voltage changes over time; larger \(R\) or \(C\) produces a longer time scale. Ideal stored energy is \(E=\tfrac12CV^2\), showing why voltage matters quadratically. This course uses only small low-voltage components, yet treats even them as charged until measured and resistor-discharged.}

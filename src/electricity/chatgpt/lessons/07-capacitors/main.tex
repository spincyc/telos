\section{Stage 1 — establish endpoints and polarity}

\begin{enumerate}
\item With the capacitor absent, measure the two-cell source:
  \(V_S=\rule{1.2cm}{.3pt}\) V. Remove the battery holder.
\item Build the pictured circuit with the switch open. Red probe goes to the
  capacitor \(+\) node; black probe goes to its \( - \) node.
\item Ask before closing: \textit{At \(t=0\), should \(V_C\) be nearer 0 V or
  \(V_S\)? What should the sign on the meter be?}
\item Adult installs the holder. Close S for 60 s. Record the final value
  \(V_C=\rule{1.2cm}{.3pt}\) V. It should be positive and approach \(V_S\).
\item Open S and remove the holder. Keep the resistor in the path until the
  meter reads below 0.10 V. Confirm: \checkoff\ discharged.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teloscard}{Worked time constant — calculate before judging the data}
\[
\tau=RC=(10{,}000\ \Omega)(0.001\ {\rm F})=10\ {\rm s}.
\qquad
V_C(\tau)=V_S(1-e^{-1})\approx0.632V_S.
\]
If \(V_S=3.00\) V, the 10 s target is \(1.90\) V. At \(5\tau=50\) s,
\(V_C\approx0.993V_S\), close to the endpoint but never an instantaneous jump.
Measured 10 s value: \rule{1.2cm}{.3pt} V. Difference from target:
\rule{1.2cm}{.3pt} V.

\section{Explain from the evidence}

Capacitance is \(C=Q/V\). During charge, the initially large difference
\(V_S-V_C\) drives current through \(R\); as \(V_C\) rises, that difference and
the current shrink. During discharge, \(V_C\) itself drives current through the
resistor, so the voltage falls exponentially without reversing polarity:
\[
V_{\rm charge}=V_S(1-e^{-t/RC}),\qquad
V_{\rm discharge}=V_0e^{-t/RC}.
\]
Ideal stored energy is \(E=\tfrac12CV^2\). For 1000 \(\mu\)F at 3.00 V,
\(E=0.0045\) J. The small value does not cancel the safe habit: treat every
capacitor as charged until it is resistor-discharged and measured.

\begin{teloscard}{Final proof — prediction, pattern, control, conclusion}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=.08em,topsep=.08em]
\item \textbf{Endpoint proof:} \(V_C(0)\approx\) \rule{1cm}{.3pt} V and
  \(V_C(5\tau)\approx\) \rule{1cm}{.3pt} V, not an instant jump.
\item \textbf{Shape proof:} equal time steps produced
  \checkoff\ equal voltage changes \quad \checkoff\ progressively smaller changes.
\item \textbf{Time-constant proof:} at \(t=RC\), charge was
  \rule{1cm}{.3pt}\(V_S\) and discharge was \rule{1cm}{.3pt}\(V_0\).
\item \textbf{Controlled-change proof:} \(22/10=2.2\); the measured time ratio
  was \rule{1cm}{.3pt}. This \checkoff\ supports \quad \checkoff\ does not support
  \(\tau\propto R\).
\item \textbf{Polarity proof:} during discharge the sign
  \checkoff\ stayed positive \quad \checkoff\ reversed.
  \par\noindent Conclusion: \rule{.76\linewidth}{.3pt}
\end{enumerate}
\end{teloscard}

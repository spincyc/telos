\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item Prepare two equal tape strips the same way; lift and suspend them side by side. Record attraction or repulsion.\item Stack two fresh strips, lift together, then separate. Compare top-to-top, bottom-to-bottom, and top-to-bottom.\item Charge a comb; approach a neutral can without touching and map the distance at which it begins to roll.\item Repeat three times, changing only distance.\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{Like charge signs repel and unlike signs attract. Rubbing and peeling redistribute charge rather than creating net charge. A charged object can attract a neutral object because charges in the neutral material shift slightly; the nearer opposite response can dominate. Humidity can drain charge, so a weak result is evidence to record, not permission to invent a result.}
\def\extension{Compare dry and humid conditions, or two insulating materials. Keep geometry and preparation count fixed.}

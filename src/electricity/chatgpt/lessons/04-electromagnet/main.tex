\def\prediction{Which should change lifting strength more: doubling turns or reversing battery polarity?}
\def\diagram{\coildiagram}
\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item Wind 20 tidy turns on the bolt and secure them. Strip only the lead ends.\item Energize for five seconds; count identical paper clips lifted. Disconnect and check temperature.\item Repeat three times, then make 40 turns with the same wire, core, source, and test.\item Use a compass to map polarity; reverse the source and repeat.\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{Current produces a magnetic field around a conductor. Coil turns reinforce the axial field; an iron core responds strongly and concentrates flux. Reversing current reverses the poles but need not greatly change strength. More current can mean more field and also more \(I^2R\) heating, so brief timed trials matter.}
\def\extension{Compare air and iron cores while holding turns and current fixed. Report both lifting result and uncertainty.}

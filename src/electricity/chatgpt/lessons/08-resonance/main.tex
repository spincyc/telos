\def\procedure{\small\begin{enumerate}\item Measure ten free oscillations and calculate period.\item Apply equal small pushes once per cycle; count cycles needed to reach a marked amplitude.\item Repeat with pushes deliberately early or late.\item Add gentle damping with a paper vane and repeat.\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{\small Resonance is a large response when drive timing matches a system's natural exchange and losses are modest. In an ideal LC circuit, energy alternates between the capacitor's electric field and inductor's magnetic field. Resistance dissipates energy and broadens/reduces the response. Resonance selects and amplifies response; it does not create energy.}
\def\extension{\small Make a qualitative response curve: drive timing on the horizontal axis, achieved amplitude on the vertical.}

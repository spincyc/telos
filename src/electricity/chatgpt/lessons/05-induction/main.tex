\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item Connect the unpowered coil to the voltmeter. Hold the magnet still far away, then still inside.\item Move one pole in at slow and fast repeatable speeds; record sign and peak.\item Pull it out along the same path. Flip the magnet and repeat.\item Hold speed approximately fixed and compare one coil with two series coils.\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{Induced emf follows change in magnetic flux linked through the circuit. No change means no sustained induced signal. Faster change and more linked turns generally increase the response. Reversing motion or pole reverses the sign because the induced effect opposes the change that produces it: Lenz's law and energy conservation tell the same story.}
\def\extension{Drop the magnet through a nonconducting guide so speed and path are reproducible; sketch voltage versus time.}

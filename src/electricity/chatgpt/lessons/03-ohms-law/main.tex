\def\procedure{\begin{enumerate}\item With power off, build source--resistor--switch in one closed path. Adult checks meter jack and range.\item Place the ammeter in the path; record current. Disconnect.\item Put the voltmeter across the resistor; reconnect briefly and record voltage.\item Repeat with three resistors. Calculate \(R=V/I\) and \(P=VI\); graph \(V\) against \(I\).\end{enumerate}}
\def\explanation{Current is charge per time; voltage describes energy transfer per charge. For an ohmic part at stable temperature, \(V=IR\). A voltmeter compares two points and an ammeter joins the current path. Putting an ammeter directly across a source makes a short circuit. Power \(P=VI\) tracks energy transfer rate, not a substance consumed by the first component.}
\def\extension{Add a second resistor in series, then in parallel. Predict equivalent resistance before measuring.}

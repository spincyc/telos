\newcommand{\fixedgates}{%
  \begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item Legality and landowner/site permission confirmed for this location.
  \item Walked, empty range; positive earthen backstop; no roads, buildings,
    trails, animals, property boundary, or unseen ground in the line of fire.
  \item Pressure-marked solid-wall pipe and pressure fittings only: no DWV,
    cellular/foam core, mixed system, damage, whitening, cracks, or UV
    embrittlement. A pressure mark is a screen, not combustion approval.
  \item Solvent joints cured at least 48 hours, and longer whenever the actual
    cement label/chart, temperature, humidity, diameter, or service demands it.
  \item No pneumatic conversion, compressed air, oxygen enrichment, liquid
    accelerant, improvised fuel transfer, or firing in cold, fire-risk, or
    prohibited conditions.
  \item Every person behind the marked line with impact-rated side-protection
    eyewear and correctly fitted earplugs \emph{plus} earmuffs.
  \end{enumerate}}

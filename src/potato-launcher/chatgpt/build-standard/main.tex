\begin{teloshazard}{Scope boundary}
This record does not approve PVC for combustion service and does not replace
component, primer, cement, or local-code instructions. It preserves conservative
minimums for an existing project: pressure-marked solid-wall components,
48 hours or the manufacturer's longer cure, no compressed gas, and retirement
on damage. If any instruction conflicts, choose the safer requirement or stop.
\end{teloshazard}

\section{Hold point B · Work that can still be inspected}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Adult cuts square, deburrs inside and out, chamfers as required by the
manufacturer, and dry-fits without forcing or modifying sockets.
\item Adult follows the actual primer/cement label for preparation, application,
assembly, twist/hold, ventilation, temperature and cleanup. No generic summary
overrides that label.
\item Mark each joint with a witness line. Before the next step hides it, record
full insertion, alignment, and a continuous visible result; reject a doubtful
joint rather than adding cement, epoxy, tape, or a patch.
\item Drilling and ignition hardware are adult-only. Any crack, craze, melted
area, distorted hole or uncertain seal rejects the part.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Hold point D · Session inspection}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\textbf{Retire immediately}
\begin{itemize}
\item crack, craze, whitening, bulge or deformation;
\item impact/drop or heat damage;
\item loose hardware or changed witness line;
\item significant UV weathering or illegible marking;
\item any prior chamber anomaly.
\end{itemize}
\columnbreak
\textbf{Release only when}
\begin{itemize}
\item build record is complete;
\item all surfaces and joints pass bright-light inspection;
\item cleanout and ignition controls function cold;
\item range, PPE, fire and legal gates pass;
\item the Range Command Card is physically present.
\end{itemize}
\end{multicols}

\begin{teloshazard}{Forbidden conversions and repairs}
Never connect compressed air, a compressor, bottled gas, or oxygen. Never use a
liquid accelerant. Never patch, sleeve, tape, re-cement, heat-straighten, or
otherwise return a rejected pressure-bearing part to service. Mark it
``RETIRED'' and render it unusable under adult control.
\end{teloshazard}

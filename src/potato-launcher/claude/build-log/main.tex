\begin{gate}{Stop here if any line does not say pressure-rated}
Every pressure-bearing part must read \textbf{Schedule 40} with an
\textbf{NSF-PW} rating and a pressure figure. If a part says DWV, cellular core,
foam core, or carries no pressure rating at all, it does not go in this build.
That pipe fails by shattering rather than splitting, and no amount of care
elsewhere compensates.
\end{gate}

\begin{telospanel}{Why a form, and not just care}
Because memory is confident and wrong. ``I'm fairly sure we glued it Friday'' is
not a cure time, and the person saying it is not lying --- they genuinely believe
it. A written date removes the argument entirely, and it removes it in advance,
which is when removing arguments is useful. Every serious workshop, laboratory
and aircraft hangar in the world runs on exactly this idea.
\end{telospanel}

\telosmasthead[The rules that do not bend]

\begin{teloshazard}{What you are building}
A potato leaving a combustion launcher carries energy in the same range as a
handgun bullet --- less speed, far more mass. It will travel several hundred
feet, it will go through a window, a fence panel or a windscreen, and it will
kill a person or an animal it hits. The chamber holds a pressure spike of
several atmospheres for a few thousandths of a second. Treat both ends of this
object with the seriousness they have earned.
\end{teloshazard}
\safetyplate

\section{The six rules}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.06]
\telosfact{1}{\textbf{One adult owns the fuel and the igniter.} Named in advance, and it does not rotate. Nobody else carries the fuel can, sprays the chamber, or presses the igniter. Ever.}
\telosfact{2}{\textbf{Muzzle discipline, exactly as for a rifle.} The muzzle points downrange or at the ground, at all times, loaded or not, fuelled or not. Never swept across a person. Never rested pointing at anything you would mind destroying. There is no such thing as an unloaded launcher.}
\telosfact{3}{\textbf{Confirm the range is clear before every shot} --- and mean the whole flight path, not just the first fifty yards. A potato at 45 degrees can carry several hundred feet and it does not stop at your property line.}
\telosfact{4}{\textbf{Everyone behind the firing line.} Nobody crosses it while
the launcher is loaded, fuelled, hot, or in a misfire hold.}
\telosfact{5}{\textbf{Never look down the barrel, and never stand beside the chamber.} If it fails, the chamber is what fails, and it fails sideways. The person firing stands behind the breech end, not level with it.}
\telosfact{6}{\textbf{A misfire is not over.} If it does not fire, wait a full \textbf{sixty seconds} with the muzzle downrange before anyone touches anything. Then open the cleanout to vent, and only then approach the muzzle. Delayed ignition is real and it is how people get hurt.}
\end{telosfacts}

\section{Build it safe, not just operate it safe}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.20]
\telosfact{Pressure-rated pipe only}{Pressure-marked solid-wall pipe and
pressure fittings throughout. Reject DWV, foam/cellular core, mixed systems,
missing markings, or undocumented service conditions. A pressure mark is a
screen, not combustion approval.}
\telosfact{Cure by the actual instructions}{Record cement, pipe size, assembly
time, temperature, and humidity. Use the manufacturer's cure chart and wait
longer whenever it requires longer. Forty-eight hours is a floor, not a
universal certificate.}
\telosfact{Never compressed air}{This design is for combustion only. PVC under sustained pneumatic pressure stores far more energy and fails catastrophically, and the failure mode is fragmentation. Do not connect a compressor to this, ever, no matter what you read.}
\telosfact{Temperature is a gate}{Compare actual conditions with component and
cement documentation. If that evidence is absent or conditions are marginal,
stop.}
\telosfact{Inspect before every session}{Look for crazing, whitening at the joints, star cracks, or any deformation of the chamber. Any of them retires the launcher permanently.}
\telosfact{Retire it on schedule}{PVC fatigues. This is a consumable object, not an heirloom. Rebuild the chamber periodically rather than finding out where its limit is.}
\end{telosfacts}

\section{The fuel is the part people get wrong}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.20]
\telosfact{Less than you think}{Over-fuelling is the most common cause of a burst chamber and of a launcher that will not fire. A rich mixture does not burn hotter --- it does not burn at all, because there is not enough oxygen. \sheetref{2. Combustion}}
\telosfact{No recipe here}{Can pressure, nozzle, formulation, propellant, and
temperature make ``spray seconds'' non-transferable. This publication does not
specify a fuel, dose, or tuning series.}
\telosfact{Never top up a failed shot}{If it does not fire, the chamber is already full of unburnt fuel. Adding more guarantees a rich mixture that still will not fire, and eventually a chamber full of fuel that fires all at once. Vent it, fan it out, start again.}
\telosfact{Never an accelerant substitution}{No starter fluid, petrol, oxygen
enrichment, torch bottle, liquid accelerant, or improvised transfer.}
\telosfact{Residue builds up}{Hairspray leaves a sticky film that collects and eventually interferes with ignition. Wipe the chamber out through the cleanout regularly.}
\telosfact{Store fuel away from the launcher}{And well away from the firing line and any source of ignition.}
\end{telosfacts}

\section{Choosing the ground}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.20]
\telosfact{Clear distance}{Several hundred yards of open ground downrange, with a wide margin either side. Assume the thing will fly twice as far as you expect, because one day it will.}
\telosfact{A positive backstop}{A verified earth bank or suitable hillside.
Trees, brush, fencing, and a calculated landing point are not backstops.}
\telosfact{Nothing downrange}{No roads, dwellings, outbuildings, livestock, footpaths or property lines in the cone of fire. Walk it before the first shot of the day.}
\telosfact{Know where it lands}{Have a spotter, and do not fire the next one until the last one has been accounted for.}
\telosfact{Fire risk}{An ignition source and dry grass. Do not fire in drought conditions, and keep water or an extinguisher on the line.}
\telosfact{Check the law}{These are legal in most of the United States and restricted in some places, and local ordinance may differ from state law. Confirm before you build, not after somebody complains.}
\end{telosfacts}

\section{Before every shot --- read it aloud}
\begin{telosframe}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=0.1em]
\item Is the range walked and clear, all the way to the backstop?
\item Is everybody behind the firing line and do they all know a shot is coming?
\item Eyes and ears on, everyone?
\item Is the chamber inspected, and is the cleanout screwed fully home?
\item Is the muzzle pointing downrange, and has it been the whole time?
\item Is this the measured fuel dose, and is this the first dose for this shot?
\item Is the last projectile accounted for?
\item Am I the named adult, and am I the one about to press the igniter?
\end{enumerate}
\end{telosframe}

\begin{teloshazard}{Misfire disposition}
Call ``MISFIRE---HOLD,'' keep the muzzle downrange, and keep everyone behind
the line for a full 60 seconds. Do not retry, add fuel, move, open, peer into,
or approach. Then the adult vents from the breech end while staying out of the
muzzle line. A second anomaly ends the session.
\end{teloshazard}

\begin{teloshazard}{If the chamber ever fails}
Stop for the day. Do not repair it, do not glue it, do not tape it. A PVC
chamber that has split or crazed once has told you its history of stress, and
the next failure will be worse. Cut it up so it cannot be reused, and build a
new one --- which is cheap, and is the reason the design is made of plumbing
parts in the first place.
\end{teloshazard}

\telosmasthead[Combustion-driven, PVC, adult-supervised]

A combustion potato launcher is a heat engine that runs once. Fuel and air burn
in a closed chamber, the gas gets hot, hot gas pushes hard, and the only thing
free to move is the potato. Every lesson here is one term in that sentence, and
each one is measured rather than asserted --- because the difference between a
launcher that works and one that bursts is entirely a matter of numbers you can
check beforehand.

\section{The craft track --- run this alongside the lessons}
The lessons explain why the launcher works. The craft sheets teach how to build
it \emph{well}, and they are the part that transfers to everything else either
of them ever makes.

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.045\linewidth}p{0.24\linewidth}L@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{\#} & \textbf{Craft sheet} & \textbf{The standard it sets}\\
\midrule
1 & Measure \& Mark & One thin line, drawn square all the way round, from a measurement taken twice.\\
2 & Cut Square & Perpendicular within a degree. No light under a try square, checked in four positions.\\
3 & Deburr \& Chamfer & No burr anywhere; an even 1--2\,mm chamfer on every pipe end.\\
4 & Solvent Weld & A continuous squeeze-out bead all the way round, pipe bottomed, witness mark aligned, date written on the part.\\
5 & Drill \& Seal & Round holes, no crazing, epoxy fillet continuous --- and the spark tested before anything is sealed.\\
6 & Inspect Your Own Work & Ten named checks, run before the step that hides the evidence, fixed rather than accepted.\\
--- & \textbf{Build Log} & The written record that turns ``I think it cured'' into a date and a signature.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{telospanel}{Why the craft track exists}
A launcher built to these standards is safe because it is \emph{sound}, not
because everyone was careful on the day. The difference matters: care varies with
mood, tiredness and how many people are waiting. A method with written
checkpoints does not. That is the whole reason trades, laboratories and hangars
work this way, and it is worth more than the launcher.
\end{telospanel}

\section{How each lesson runs}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.20]
\telosfact{Predict first}{Every lesson opens with a question and a box to commit an answer in, before anything is lit. A wrong prediction written down teaches far more than a demonstration nobody doubted.}
\telosfact{They build it}{The boys cut, deburr, dry-fit, glue and assemble. The adult owns fuel, ignition and the decision to fire.}
\telosfact{Measure it}{Every lesson produces a number: a pressure, a ratio, a distance, a velocity. Numbers are what separate this from a stunt.}
\telosfact{Break it}{Change one variable and predict the change. Twice the fuel. Half the barrel. A loose potato.}
\telosfact{Then the maths}{The equation arrives as the compact way to state what they already measured.}
\end{telosfacts}

\section{Who does what}
\begin{telosfacts}[0.20]
\telosfact{Lessons 1--4}{Bench work: a can, a candle, a balloon, a tape measure. Nothing here is dangerous beyond ordinary kitchen care.}
\telosfact{The build}{The boys do essentially all of it. Cutting, deburring, solvent welding and the 48-hour cure are the bulk of the work and none of it involves fuel.}
\telosfact{Firing}{\textbf{The adult is in charge of the fuel can, the igniter and the command to fire, permanently.} Everyone else is behind the firing line.}
\telosfact{Where}{Open rural ground with several hundred yards clear downrange, a backstop, and nothing and nobody in the line of fire. This is not a backyard device.}
\telosfact{Before anything}{Read the \textbf{Safety} sheet start to finish, and check local ordinances --- these are legal in most places and restricted in some, and that is on you to confirm before you build.}
\end{telosfacts}

\pvcnote

\begin{telospanel}{The one sentence version}
A measured amount of fuel mixed with the right amount of air burns in a fraction
of a second; the gas in the chamber roughly triples in pressure; that pressure
acts on the base of the potato all the way down the barrel; and the potato
leaves the muzzle carrying the energy the fuel released. Lessons 1 to 4 are the
four clauses of that sentence.
\end{telospanel}

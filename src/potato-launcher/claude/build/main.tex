\begin{telospanel}{The standard}
Do not reward a finished-looking launcher. Reward a documented sequence of
square cuts, clean sockets, fully seated joints, a complete cure, and an
adult's final decision. A doubtful pressure part is not repaired or ``good
enough'': mark it \textsc{reject}, make it unusable, and replace it.
\end{telospanel}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Place every dry, uncemented part in order. Match the printed ratings to
the parts list. Photograph or sketch the rating marks before they disappear
under fittings.
\item Measure the depth of each socket with a rule. Mark insertion depth on
each pipe end, then draw an alignment line across each future joint.
\item Measure the pipe twice at points 90 degrees apart. If the readings
differ, stop: the end is out of round.
\item Dry-fit without forcing. Every pipe must reach its depth mark. Never
``fix'' a loose fit with extra cement.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Clamp the pipe without crushing it. Cut at the waste side of the mark,
using a mitre box or pipe cutter. Do not hurry the final stroke.
\item Deburr the full circumference inside and out. Make only a small chamfer;
the pressure wall must remain full thickness.
\item Hold a square at four positions around the end. Shine a light behind it.
Record the largest visible gap with a feeler gauge or rule.
\item Wipe the end clean with a dry white cloth. Inspect under raking light.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teloshazard}{Remake decision}
If an end is visibly slanted, chipped, cracked, deeply scored, heat-damaged, or
too short after correction, do not advance it. Mark the defect, cut back to
sound material if the planned dimensions still permit, or replace the pipe.
Never fill a bad cut with cement or epoxy.
\end{teloshazard}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Mark hole centers on the agreed chamber location. Confirm that hardware
will clear the cleanout, reducer, and hands.
\item Support the inside of the pipe. Drill small pilot holes, then only to the
diameter required by the fasteners. Deburr without enlarging them.
\item Install washers and nuts. Set the internal electrode gap to 3--5\,mm.
Seal only as specified on the craft sheet; sealant does not compensate for a
loose or oversized hole.
\item With the cleanout open, chamber empty, and no fuel present, have the
adult operate the piezo. Observe from the open end, never over the chamber.
\end{enumerate}

\end{tikzpicture}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Rehearse the complete movement dry: orient, insert fully, quarter-turn,
hold. Assign one adult to read time and one to witness the marks.
\item Work one joint at a time in ventilation specified by the product labels.
Prime both mating surfaces, then cement both; follow the manufacturer's
application and temperature instructions.
\item Push to the socket-depth mark, turn a quarter turn, restore the alignment
mark, and hold for 30 seconds. Do not disturb the joint.
\item Wipe only the exterior excess. Never scrape, heat, pressure-test, or
``exercise'' a fresh joint.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teloshazard}{Stop, do not patch}
Reject a joint that backs out, misses its depth mark, rotates away from its
alignment mark, contains a visible dry area, or was assembled after the cement
had skinned. Do not add cement around the outside. Retire the affected pressure
part and remake the assembly. \sheetref{04 Solvent Weld}

\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Write assembly date and time on the chamber. Place it horizontally on
two broad supports where nobody will lift, twist, load, or test it.
\item Leave it at least 48 hours, and longer if the cement label requires it
for the actual pipe size, temperature, or humidity. The label governs.
\item After cure, inspect every joint in bright raking light. Turn the assembly
slowly. Look for a continuous seated shoulder, alignment, cracks, whitening,
bulges, gaps, and disturbed witness marks.
\item Inspect the cleanout threads dry. The plug must run fully home by hand
without cross-threading. Never glue, tape over, or force damaged threads.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{teloshazard}{No improvised pressure proof}
Do not plug the barrel, add compressed air, hydrostatically pressurize this
assembly, strike it, heat it, or perform a close-range ``test shot.'' Those
tests introduce hazards and do not convert questionable workmanship into
proof. A failed inspection means retire and remake.
\end{teloshazard}

\section{Operation is a separate gate}
This build record gives no permission to fire. Before any use, the supervising
adult must apply the \textbf{Safety} sheet, local law, landowner rules, weather,
backstop, exclusion zone, eye and hearing protection, muzzle control, and
misfire procedure. Use only the single approved operating procedure; do not
vary fuel, projectile fit, barrel, angle, or other variables to seek more
pressure, speed, or range. If performance seems abnormal, stop and inspect;
never ``tune'' a combustion launcher.
